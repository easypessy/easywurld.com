# Easywurld — 30 Day Social Plan
### 11 core posts a day, restructured for TikTok, Instagram and Facebook

**Every post is ready to publish.** Hook, beats, voiceover, slide copy, caption, hashtags and
visual direction are all written. No placeholders, nothing to decide on the day.

| | |
|---|---|
| Plan starts | **Sunday 1 November 2026** (change it in the app if you want a different month) |
| Ends | **Monday 2 November 2026** |
| Daily volume | 11 core posts → 11 TikTok + 11 Instagram + 11 Facebook = **33 posts a day** |
| Month total | **66 posts** (22 core ideas x 3 platforms) |
| Times | Africa/Lagos (WAT, UTC+1). Every post block carries its own time. |
| Phone tool | `EASYWURLD_POST_COMMANDER.html` — one file, works offline, copy button on every block, tick marks that save on the phone. |
| Spreadsheet | `content_calendar.csv` — one row per post, dated, editable on your phone in Google Sheets. |
| Brand rules | `marketing/brand-prompt.md`. Never invent results, ratings or testimonials. |

**How to read a post block.** Each post has one core idea and up to four platform versions.
The platform versions are different writing, not the same caption pasted four times.

**The daily mix.** 7 entertain, 3 teach, 1 sell — one job per post, never two.
Memes sit near 20% of output.

**Scheduling a month ahead.** Everything below is dated. Open the sheet, sort by date, and
schedule the whole month in one sitting. The app is for the days when something needs
writing on the spot.

# How to publish this from your phone

**One file.** `EASYWURLD_POST_COMMANDER.html` is a single file, works with no internet, and
saves your tick marks on the phone. Add it to your home screen and it behaves like an app.

**Free scheduling, no new apps.**

| Platform | How to schedule | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram + Facebook | Meta Business Suite, timezone Africa/Lagos | Free |
| TikTok | Schedule option inside the app for business accounts | Free |
| LinkedIn | The schedule button in the post composer | Free |

**Set the whole month in one sitting.** Every post above is dated, so the first Sunday of the
month you can schedule all thirty days at once. After that it is 20 minutes a week. Leave the
two slots a week that need a real human reply open, and do not schedule a post you have not
read out loud once.

**Where to schedule from.** `content_calendar.csv` is the same plan as a Google Sheet. Sort by
date, and every row carries its time, its format and the full copy, so you never have to
remember which post goes where.

**Storage.** Keep images and videos in Google Drive, not on the phone. Open them from Drive
when you need them. Text is cheap, video is not.

**Reply first, post second.** A reply to a real customer beats one more post. If a slot
arrives and a customer is waiting, answer the customer and let the post wait.
