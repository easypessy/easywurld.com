# Easywurld launch content (posts only)

## New: the phone-first social kit

[`social-command/`](./social-command/) is the current version, built for posting from a phone.

- **`EASYWURLD_POST_COMMANDER.html`** — one file, works offline, opens on today's day of the
  week with the next post due, and has a Month tab with the whole 30-day grid. Copy buttons
  for every platform version, tick marks that save on the phone. About 1 MB, so it will not
  fill a phone.
- **`MONTH_POSTS.md`** — 30 days x 11 core posts, each restructured for TikTok, Instagram,
  Facebook and LinkedIn. 1,140 posts with hooks, scripts, captions, hashtags and visual
  directions, every one dated.
- **`content_calendar.csv`** — 1,140 rows, one per scheduled post, dated, with Lagos times.
- **`POSTING_PLAN.md`** — posting times for Nigeria, why 37 posts a day is allowed, what makes
  writing sound human, the emotion map, meme rules, and the phone workflow.
- **`MEME_BANK.md`** — all 37 memes in the month, already scheduled, with the exact words.
- **`CLAUDE_OFFERS_PROMPT.md`** — the prompt to give Claude to generate the offers PDF.
- **`Easywurld Offers Master.pdf`** — the printed offers booklet.

Everything above is also in Google Drive, in the `Easywurld` folder.

## Get everything in ONE click

1. Click **[Easywurld_Posts_By_Day.zip](Easywurld_Posts_By_Day.zip)** (in this folder).
2. Click the **download icon** (down arrow) at the top right of the file page.
3. Open the ZIP on your laptop and choose **Extract all**.

You get `Day_01` to `Day_30`. Inside each day there is one folder per post, in posting order:

```
Day_01/
  Day_01_Plan.txt                      what to post today, with times
  Post_01_0800_Instagram-and-Facebook-feed_.../
      Slide_1.png ... Slide_6.png      (or Image.png, or Video.mp4)
      Caption.txt                      copy and paste this
  Post_02_0900_WhatsApp-Status_.../
  ...
```

Every post folder holds its image or video and its caption together, so you never download images one by one.

## Where the paid product files live

The nine ₦5,000 offer deliverable packs are **not in this public repository**. They are generated by
the `packkit` tool and kept in a private repository, because they are the products customers pay for.

This repository keeps only what is safe to be public: the launch content in this folder, the marketing
notes in `marketing/`, the site itself, and the offer descriptions in `client/src/data/offers.ts`.

`.gitignore` blocks the pack output paths (`offer-deliverables/`, `Easywurld-Offers-Deliverables.zip`,
`offers-summary.md`, `tools/packkit/`) so they cannot be committed here by accident.
