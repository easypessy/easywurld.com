A proposal is not a longer quote. It is a short argument: here is your problem, here is my plan, here is the price, here is how we start.

I keep a few rules:
1. Page one holds the summary, price, date and next step.
2. Scope says what is included and what is not.
3. Price comes as options when possible.
4. I follow up on day 2, 5 and 10.
