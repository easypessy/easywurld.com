A homepage has about ten seconds to answer three questions: what do you do, who is it for, and what should I do next?

I keep seeing small business websites that answer none of them in the first screen. “Welcome to our website” and a photo of a handshake. The owner is proud of the business and the visitor leaves confused.

You can fix most of this with words, not a rebuild:
1. A headline that says what you do and for whom.
2. One supporting line.
3. One main button.
