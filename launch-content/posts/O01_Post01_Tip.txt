Someone asks “how much?” and you reply with a number. Then silence.

Try three parts instead:
1. The price
2. What it includes (one line)
3. A question that moves things forward

“The Ankara set is ₦18,000, tailored to your size, ready in 5 days. Would you like me to take your measurements today?”

A price on its own is a dead end. A price with a next step is a conversation.
