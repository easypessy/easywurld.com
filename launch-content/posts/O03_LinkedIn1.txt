Consistency is the hard part of content for a one-person business. Not ideas, not tools: consistency.

What has helped me is a simple structure:
- Four pillars: Teach, Show, Trust, Sell.
- One batching session a week.
- A 30-day calendar I can see.

When the calendar is full, there is no daily “what should I post?” moment.

I turned this into the 30-Day Content Engine (₦5,000): a guide, a 60-idea post bank, an auto-dated calendar and a swipe file with 25 hooks. It is a planning system, not finished posts, and it cannot promise reach.
