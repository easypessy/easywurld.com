# START HERE — the 30-day drop

**What this is.** Thirty zips. One per posting day, starting **Wednesday 21 October 2026**
and ending **Thursday 19 November 2026**. Every zip holds that day's finished images and
videos, the caption to paste, and the order to post them in. Nothing to design, nothing to
write on the night.

**The routine, every night — four minutes:**

1. Open **01 UNZIP ONE A NIGHT** and download the zip for tomorrow's date.
2. Unzip it. You get numbered folders, 01 to 40. The number is the clock order.
3. Open `00_START_HERE.txt` inside. It lists the day at a glance.
4. For each folder: upload the picture or video, copy `caption.txt`, paste, post.
5. Delete the zip from your phone when the day is done. Go to bed.

The zip names carry the day *and* the date, so you never have to work out which one is next:
`Day 01 · Wed 21 Oct`, `Day 02 · Thu 22 Oct`, and so on.

## The honest part

The plan asks for 38 pieces of output a day when every platform version is counted, because
that is what the written plan always said. Nobody sustains that from a phone for thirty days
and keeps a business running. So this drop is arranged so you can do **less without breaking
the chain**:

- **Full day (about 25 minutes on the phone):** post everything in order.
- **Ten-minute day:** post the offer post, one teaching post, and the 21:45 Story. That keeps
  the account alive and selling. Skip the rest without guilt.
- **Zero day:** the 21:45 Story only. One image, one link.

The month does not collapse if you miss a meme. It collapses if you stop for a week.

## Sounds, links and the small things

- **All videos are silent on purpose.** Add a trending sound inside TikTok or Instagram before
  publishing — that way the platform never flags the file for licensed music. Captions are
  already burned into the video.
- **Links:** put `https://wa.me/2349050690837` on every Story and in the bio. Use the keyword
  (`PLAYBOOK`, `CLEAR`, `ENGINE`, `PAGE`, `LOCAL`, `MESSAGE`, `PROMPTS`, `TEMPLATES`,
  `LAUNCH`) in the first line of every caption — that is how buyers order.
- **Do not rewrite the captions on the day.** They are written in your voice, with your
  limits stated out loud. Rewriting at 9pm is how mistakes get in.
- **Photo of a real person:** if you want a human face on the feed, see *THE FACE — read this
  before you post a face* in this folder. Short version: a real face beats a synthetic one,
  and a synthetic one must never pretend to be a founder.
