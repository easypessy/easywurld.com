That is 5% on a single pack and 4.2% on the bundle. The flat ₦50 is what hurts small sales
most — it is 1% of a ₦5,000 pack and 0.2% of a ₦25,000 bundle.

## The one setting that matters

In every Selar product, turn on **"buyer pays the fee"**. Then ₦5,000 stays ₦5,000 on the page
and you keep the full amount minus the 4%.

If you would rather absorb the fee instead, do not chase a round number and end up with an
ugly price. Listing at ₦5,263 nets exactly ₦5,000 — but a page that says ₦5,263 costs more in
trust than the ₦263 it saves. Charge ₦5,000 and let the buyer pay the fee.
