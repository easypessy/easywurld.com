The most expensive website mistakes I see are not design mistakes. They are ownership mistakes.

The domain is in the developer's name. Nobody knows where the hosting login is. A renewal date passes and the site goes offline.

Three habits prevent most of this:
1. Register the domain in your own name and email.
2. Keep an inventory of accounts and renewal dates.
3. Check the site for 30 minutes a month.

I put these and more into the Website Launch & Care Handbook (₦5,000): the handbook, a planning brief, a launch, care and accounts workbook, and a hiring guide with 25 questions.

It does not build, host or maintain a site, and it is not legal advice.
