Most marketing problems in small businesses are message problems in disguise.

The website is fine, the posts go out, and customers still ask “so what exactly do you do?”

Before posting more, I would do three things:
1. Write one sentence: I help [who] get [what] by [how].
2. Talk to five real customers and note their words.
3. Put the result into one page: customer, message, channels, offers, 90-day priorities.

I built this into the Clear Message Workbook (₦5,000): workbook with a fictional worked example, a 15-question interview kit, a message bank with bios and pitches, and a one-page plan. It is self-guided, and it cannot promise leads.
