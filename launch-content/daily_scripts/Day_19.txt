Most small businesses in Nigeria do not lose sales on price. They lose them in the gap between “how much?” and the second reply.

A lot of selling happens in WhatsApp and Instagram DMs, and most of us handle it from memory. A quote goes out, the customer goes quiet, and the thread sinks under ten newer ones.

Three habits fix a surprising amount of this:
1. Reply with price, what it includes, and a next step.
2. Follow up on day 1, 3 and 7.
3. Write the follow-up date down.

I put these into a short playbook with 36 scripts and a tracker: the Chat-to-Customer Playbook, ₦5,000. It will not create enquiries or guarantee sales. It helps you handle the ones you already have.

“Hi” is not a reply. Neither is “Yes available.”

A better first reply:
- Greet and use their name
- Answer the question they asked
- Ask one question to learn what they need

It takes ten seconds longer and makes you sound like a business people can trust.
