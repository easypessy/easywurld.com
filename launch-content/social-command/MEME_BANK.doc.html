Easywurld — Meme Bank

30 memes, ready to build. Two a day, in the 09:00 slot and the 20:00 slot. A meme is not a caption with a font. It is a picture and a reaction.

How to build one in 10 minutes

  1. Pick the meme below.
  2. Use any free meme generator, or Canva. Type the top text and the bottom text exactly as

written.

  1. Keep the Easywurld colours: cream #FAE7C7 background, deep green #0A4937 text, one

terracotta #9E4E2C accent if you want it. No gradients, no stock photos, no drop shadows.

  1. Post it as a plain image. Do not put a logo on it. Let the joke carry it.
  2. Do not add a sales pitch to a meme. The morning meme is context, the evening meme is the

share.

Before you post it, check three things

  • Would a customer find this funny? If it is only funny to other marketers, skip it.
  • Is it a punch down at a customer, a competitor or a country? Skip it.
  • Is the joke obvious in two seconds? If you had to think, it is a caption, not a meme.

Formats used below

  • Two panel: classic, top text and bottom text. Use this for the chat screenshots.
  • One panel: a single line of big text on cream. Use this for the industry-insider ones.
  • Chat panel: a fake chat window. Use this for the customer conversations.
  • POV: a bold statement and a small second line.

Notes on the two daily slots

Morning meme, 09:15 on TikTok / 10:00 Instagram / 10:30 Facebook. Keep it light. It is context, and it earns you the reach.

Evening meme, 20:45 / 20:45 / 21:00. This is the one that gets shared, because people share at night when they are relaxed. Put the sharpest joke here.

Never put the same meme on two platforms on the same day. Rewrite the bottom line for each platform, and never crop a meme differently on two platforms, because the same person follows you on both.
