## The daily shape

11 core posts a day, each rewritten for each platform.

| Platform | Posts | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| TikTok | 11 | Spoken, 20 to 30 seconds, hook in the first 3 seconds. |
| Instagram | 11 | 6 carousels, 3 reels, 2 static. |
| Facebook | 11 | Text posts that read like a conversation. |
| LinkedIn | 4 + 1 optional | Under 3,000 characters, professional. |
| **Total** | **37 a day, 259 a week** | |

All times are Lagos time (WAT, UTC+1) and are already in the Post Commander and the sheet.

## Three rules

1. Never post a result, ranking, rating or testimonial you cannot evidence. Demo projects are
   labelled **concept project · sample build**.
2. Value figures are hours x ₦5,000 and are described as an estimate, never as a former price.
3. Say plainly what a pack does not do. The honest post is the one that builds the business.
