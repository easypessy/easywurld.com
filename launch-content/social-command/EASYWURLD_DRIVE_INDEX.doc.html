3. The month, already dated

Monday 19 October 2026 → Tuesday 17 November 2026. 30 days.

  • 11 core posts a day
  • 11 TikTok + 11 Instagram + 11 Facebook + 4 LinkedIn + 1 optional LinkedIn = 37 posts a day
  • 1,110 scheduled posts for the month, plus 30 optional LinkedIn slots
  • Every day has a theme, an offer in focus, and every post carries its own time in Lagos time

Scheduling the whole month: open the Content Calendar, sort by date, and schedule in one sitting. Meta Business Suite for Instagram and Facebook, the native scheduler for TikTok and LinkedIn. All free. Set the timezone to Africa/Lagos.

If you want to start on a different Monday, the Post Commander has a start-date box in the Month tab. Change it and every date moves with it.
