Easywurld Social Command Kit

Start here. Everything you need is in this folder, plus one file on your phone.

The one thing to do first

Open EASYWURLD_POST_COMMANDER.html on your phone.

  • It is a single file, about a megabyte. It will not fill your phone.
  • It opens on today's day of the week, with the next post due at the top.
  • The Month tab shows the whole 30-day run on a grid, so you can plan a fortnight ahead

instead of reacting to today.

  • Every post has a copy button. Tap, copy, paste into the app. Tick it off. The tick marks

save on the phone.

  • It works with no internet.
  • Add it to your home screen and it behaves like an app.

How to add it to your home screen. Open the file in your phone browser, tap the share or menu button in the top bar, and choose "Add to Home Screen". Do this once and it is there forever, like an app, with no icon pretending to be something it is not.

The posting shape

11 core posts a day, restructured for each platform.

PostsNotes
TikTok11Short video, hook first, 20 to 30 seconds.
Instagram116 carousels, 3 reels, 2 static.
Facebook11Text posts that read like a conversation.
LinkedIn4 + 1 optionalUnder 3,000 characters, professional, about the work.
Total37 a day, 1,140 across the 30 days19 October to 17 November 2026.

The 11 core ideas are shared. The writing is not. TikTok is spoken and fast, Instagram is slides, Facebook is conversation, LinkedIn is professional. That is what stops the feed looking like a spam account.

The daily mix. 3 teaching, 2 memes, 2 honest answers, 2 offer posts, 1 story, 1 hot take. Memes stay near 20% of output.

Everything is dated. Day 1 is Monday 19 October 2026, day 30 is Tuesday 17 November

No paid tool. These are free and already on your phone.

PlatformHow

LinkedInThe schedule button in the post composer.
Everything elseThe tick marks in the Post Commander.

Batch it on Sunday night, 20 minutes. Copy what you can, schedule it, and leave open the two slots that need a real human reply. A reply to a real customer beats one more post. On the first Sunday of the month, set the whole month, because it is already written.

Keep media in Google Drive, not on the phone. Text is cheap, video is not. Open a file from Drive when you need it. Your phone's storage does not need a copy of everything.

The rules that keep it honest

  • No client results, rankings, ratings, testimonials or guarantees you cannot evidence.
  • Every demo project is labelled concept project · sample build.
  • Value figures are hours x ₦5,000, described as an estimate, never as a former price.
  • Each honest post says plainly what the pack does not do.
  • Never post a logo on a meme. Let the joke carry it.
  • If you miss a slot, skip it. Do not stack three posts at 11pm to catch up.
