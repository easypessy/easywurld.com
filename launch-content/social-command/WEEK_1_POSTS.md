# Easywurld — Week 1 Social Posts
### 11 core posts a day, restructured for TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn

**Every post in this file is ready to publish.** Hook, beats, voiceover, slide copy, caption,
hashtags and visual direction are all written. No placeholders.

| | |
|---|---|
| Daily volume | 11 core posts → 11 TikTok + 11 Instagram + 11 Facebook + 4 LinkedIn (plus 1 optional LinkedIn) = **37 posts a day** |
| Week total | **259 posts** |
| Times | Africa/Lagos (WAT, UTC+1). Times are inside every post block. |
| Phone tool | Open `EASYWURLD_POST_COMMANDER.html` on your phone. One file, works offline, copy button on every block. |
| Spreadsheet | `content_calendar.csv` opens in Google Sheets, editable on your phone. |
| Brand rules | `../brand-prompt.md` in `marketing/`. Never invent results, ratings or testimonials. |

**How to read a post block.** Each post has one core idea and up to four platform versions.
The platform versions are different pieces of writing, not the same caption pasted four times.
TikTok is spoken and fast, Instagram is slides, Facebook is conversation, LinkedIn is professional
and under 3,000 characters.

**The daily mix.** 3 teaching posts, 2 memes, 2 trust posts, 2 offer posts, 1 story, 1 hot take.
Memes stay at roughly 20% of output, which is where brands get the most reach without losing
customers.

# How to publish this from your phone

**One file.** `EASYWURLD_POST_COMMANDER.html` is about a fifth of a megabyte. Open it in your
phone browser, add it to your home screen, and it behaves like an app. It works with no
internet. Your tick marks and notes save on the phone.

**Free scheduling, no new apps.**

| Platform | How to schedule | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram + Facebook | Meta Business Suite, set timezone to Africa/Lagos | Free |
| TikTok | Schedule option inside the app for business accounts | Free |
| LinkedIn | Schedule button in the post composer | Free |

**Batch it on Sunday night, 20 minutes.** Schedule the week, then leave the two slots that
need a real human reply open. Do not schedule a post you have not read out loud once.

**Storage.** Keep the images and videos in Google Drive, not on the phone. Open them from Drive
when you need them, and delete them from the phone afterwards. Text is cheap, video is not.

**Reply first, post second.** A reply to a real customer beats one more post. If a slot arrives
and a customer is waiting, answer the customer and let the post wait.
