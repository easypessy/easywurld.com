## 🕘 09:00 — LinkedIn Personal 01

> Saturday morning reflection.
>
> I decided this year to price every Easywurld product at ₦5,000. People I respect in business told me I was underpricing. That I should have a ₦50,000 course. That I should have a ₦500,000 coaching program. That ₦5,000 was "too cheap" and would devalue what I knew.
>
> I thought about it for a long time. Here is why I didn't do that.
>
> The audience I build for is mostly small business owners in Nigeria running businesses that make between ₦50,000 and ₦500,000 a month. For most of them, ₦50,000 is a real decision. ₦500,000 is impossible. ₦5,000 is a price they can say yes to today without asking a spouse, without writing a proposal, without a budget meeting.
>
> If my work is good — and I believe it is — ₦5,000 is the price that gets it into the hands of the people who actually need it, right now, when they need it, instead of six months from now when they've "saved up".
>
> I would rather sell 1,000 ₦5,000 products to 1,000 real founders than one ₦5,000,000 product to one person who can afford it. The work matters more to me at the ₦5,000 level because that is where the leaks are actually killing businesses.
>
> Easywurld stays ₦5,000 per product. No ₦500k upsell. No pressure. Just ₦5,000 tools that fix real leaks. That is my commitment for as long as the business runs.
