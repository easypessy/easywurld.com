## 🕧 12:30 — LinkedIn Business 03

**Headline:** The AI safety checklist I run before I press send on anything AI drafted.

> Eight checks, in 30 seconds:
>
> 1. Does it name any facts or numbers I didn't verify?
> 2. Does it promise anything we don't actually do?
> 3. Does it use a word I would never actually say ("delighted", "seamless")?
> 4. Does it sound like it could have been written by any business in my category?
> 5. If the customer replied with the hardest possible question, does this reply still hold up?
> 6. Did I add one thing specific to this customer?
> 7. Am I hiding behind formal language instead of being direct?
> 8. Would I be comfortable saying this out loud to the person's face?
>
> If any answer is wrong, fix it before sending. If all eight pass, press send.
>
> You don't need a compliance department. You need 30 seconds and eight honest questions.
>
> #AI #Safety #SmallBusiness
