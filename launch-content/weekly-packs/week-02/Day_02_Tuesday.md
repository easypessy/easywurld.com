## 🕘 09:00 — LinkedIn Personal 01

> I realised something this week about why I have always found writing easy but found posting hard.
>
> Writing 11 posts a day is not 11 times harder than writing one post. In fact, once the decision about what type of post it is has been made, each post gets easier. The first post of the day is the hardest. The eleventh comes out quickly.
>
> The hard part is the decision. What type of post should it be? What angle? What emotion? Once that is decided, the writing is just typing.
>
> This is true of most creative work. It is not the doing that is expensive in energy. It is the deciding.
>
> Anything you can do to move the decision to a time when you are not tired — Sunday evening, for planning — makes the doing on a tired Tuesday morning vastly easier.
