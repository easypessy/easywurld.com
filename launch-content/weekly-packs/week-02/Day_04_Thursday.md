**Core idea:** How to write an above-the-fold that passes the three-second test — the formula is "We help [audience] get [result] without [pain]. [Call to action]."
**Emotion:** Relief (there's a simple formula).
**Objective:** Education.

> with this formula. It takes 5 minutes.
