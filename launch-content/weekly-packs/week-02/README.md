## Key difference from Week 1

Week 1 was "establish the framework" — every post introduced an idea.
Week 2 goes one layer deeper:
- Diagnosis posts name more specific problems (competing on price, word-of-mouth invisibility, the "quality" trap).
- Education posts include actionable examples readers can apply the same day.
- Myth-busting tackles myths that survived Week 1 ("lower price gets more customers", "I'll start posting when I have better content", "AI is only for young people").
- Proof posts mix Easywurld's own continued tracking with additional concept demonstrations and one anonymised customer story (with permission, kept general).
- Personal stories go deeper — the client who taught me about pricing, the month I didn't post, the Saturday I said no to a big job.
- Saturday is entirely human — fewer slides, longer captions, more conversation.
- Sunday includes a two-week retrospective rather than a new pitch.
