> OPTION A: The no-tech January pre-booking.
> Just WhatsApp. No pages. No websites.

3.
> **Step 1 — Build your broadcast list today (before you close)**
> Create a WhatsApp broadcast list containing every customer who has bought from you this year (or in the last 6 months at minimum). Everyone. Even the person who bought once in March. Even the person who complained. Everyone.
>
> If you don't know how to create a broadcast list: on WhatsApp go to the top-right menu → New broadcast → select the contacts. Save the list as "Customers 2025" or similar. You'll use this same list for the Christmas thank-you (yesterday's Script 5) and for the January "we're back" message.

4.
> **Step 2 — Send the pre-booking message on 24 December morning**
> On 24 December morning, after you've sent the Christmas thank-you (wait an hour or two), send this to the same broadcast list:
>
> "Hi [Name]! Merry Christmas again. Before I close for the year, one quick note: January bookings are now open. If you'd like to book a [your thing — e.g. cake / appointment / order / slot] for the first two weeks of January, just reply to this message with your preferred date and a quick note of what you need, and I will hold a space for you. A small deposit of [your deposit amount — e.g. ₦2,000 / 50%] secures it. Rest well, see you in January! 🙏"
>
> Personalise with their name (broadcast lists let you send to many people but they see it as a personal message). Keep it short. No long explanation. No pressure. One clear action: reply with a date.

5.
> **Step 3 — Confirm and log bookings as they come in (before you close)**
> When people reply with dates (and they will — I promise you people reply), reply warmly, confirm the date, send deposit details (if you take deposits — more on this later), and write their name, date, and order straight into your booking book.
>
> Do this throughout 24 December morning until your close time. Then turn on your away message and put your phone away. Some people will reply on 25, 26, 27 Dec — your away message will hold them, and you can confirm them when you briefly check (or have a trusted person confirm if you've delegated) OR just wait until 2 January. It is fine.
>
> No automations. No funnels. No link in bio. Just conversations with people who already trust you.

6.
> **Step 4 — The 2 January confirmation broadcast**
> On the morning of 2 January when you reopen, send this to the same list:
>
> "Happy new year [Name]! We are back and rested and ready for a great year. If you booked a January slot in December, send me a quick 'hi' with your date to confirm and we will lock everything in. If you haven't booked yet there are still a few spaces left in the first two weeks — just reply with what you need and we'll sort you. Can't wait to work with you this year! 🙏"
>
> This message does three things: it confirms the people who already booked (flushes out any forgotten bookings), it gives a second chance to people who saw the 24 Dec message and didn't act, and it announces you're open in the warmest possible way — a direct personal message, not a post.

If you want a cake in January, reply to this message with the date and I'll put you down. Don't worry about paying now, we'll sort it in January. Thank you for everything this year. Merry Christmas! — Nkechi"
>
> I sent it. I put my phone down. I started baking. I forgot about it.

4.
