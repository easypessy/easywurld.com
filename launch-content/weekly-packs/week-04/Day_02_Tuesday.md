## 🕜 13:30 — LinkedIn Personal 02

**Headline:** On holding a deadline kindly.

> When I first started in business I would say "let me see what I can do" to everyone who begged for one more order past the deadline, because I hated saying no.
>
> Every single time I did that, three things happened: (1) I was up until 2am rushing, (2) the work was worse than my standard, (3) the customer still wasn't happy because they could feel it was rushed.
>
> Learning to say a clear kind "no" was one of the best business skills I ever developed. It didn't lose me customers. It kept customers. It earned me respect. It let me sleep.
>
> If you are holding deadlines this week and feeling guilty, let this be your permission: quality is a promise you make to everyone who booked on time. A kind no honours that promise. It isn't rude. It's professional.
