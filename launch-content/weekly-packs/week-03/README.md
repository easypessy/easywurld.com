# Week 03 · 23 November – 29 November 2026 · Customers Who Come Back

## Week 3 arc
After two weeks of foundations (Week 1 diagnosis/system, Week 2 fixing the four big leaks), Week 3 shifts focus to **the gold mine most businesses ignore** — existing customers, repeat business, referrals, and preparing for the December/end-of-year rush. The theme: **a customer who has already bought from you is 7x more likely to buy again than a stranger is to buy for the first time — but most businesses spend 100% of their energy chasing strangers.**

This is also the week we lean into the calendar: December is the busiest and most profitable 6 weeks for most Nigerian small businesses, and we prepare for it now before the rush hits.

### Emotional anchor for the week
**Warmth** — this week is about relationships, not systems. Customers are people. Remember them, thank them, treat them like humans, and they will come back.

### Key Week 3 angles
- Repeat customers beat new customers 7-to-1 — and they are free.
- Asking for referrals is not pushy when you ask well.
- The Christmas "thank you" message is the highest-ROI message of the year.
- A simple customer list (name / what they bought / date) is gold in January.
- December content needs to feel like December — warmer, more grateful, more gift-focused, less selling.
- Preparing January now means you don't start the year with an empty pipeline.
- For local/walk-in businesses: being remembered by name is the single biggest competitive advantage over any big brand.
- Rest is even more important in December — the rush will eat you if you don't protect your time.
