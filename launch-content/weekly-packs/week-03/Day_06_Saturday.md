> /deadline, /wrap, /exchange, /closed, /january. You can copy the scripts from Wednesday's post or use the templates in the Playbook.
>
> **3. Write your post-purchase thank-you message (10 minutes)**
> One warm message that goes to every customer who buys in December: "Thank you so much — I really appreciate you choosing me this Christmas. Your order is being prepared / will be delivered by X. Have a wonderful week."

5.
> **4. Set your away message and closed dates now (10 minutes)**
> Don't wait until 23 December to decide when you're closing. Pick your closed days (recommend 24 Dec – 2 Jan, or whatever works for your family), put them in your away message now so people know what to expect.
>
> **5. Batch 3 days of content (30 minutes)**
> Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday. One slide-1 per day, captions for day 1, outlines for days 2 and 3. That gets you through the first half of the week without the "what do I post" panic.

6.
> **6. Send two past-customer check-ins (5 minutes)**
> Look at your Regulars Book or customer list. Pick two people you haven't spoken to in a while. Send them a "just wanted to say hello, December slots are open if you need anything" message. Two messages. That's 2 minutes.
>
> **7. Set an 8pm alarm (5 minutes)**
> Put a recurring alarm on your phone for 8pm every weeknight in December. When it goes off, you close the laptop. You finish whatever you're doing and you stop. Tired brains make bad decisions after 8pm, especially in December.
