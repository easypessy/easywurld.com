> When: 90 days after purchase, when you are about to launch something relevant to what they bought.

> Why it works: it's exclusive, it's personal, it references what they bought, and it's a heads-up, not a sell. It makes them feel like a VIP, not a name on a list.

5.
> Message 3 — The 6-month thank-you
> When: every 6 months, or at the end of the year.
>

I'm putting together a small thank-you for past customers this December — let me know if this WhatsApp number is still the best one to reach you on."
>
> Why it works: it's genuine gratitude, not a sell. It confirms their contact details. It sets up a future offer without pitching right now.

> · Don't ask for a referral. Don't push a discount. Don't invite them to follow your page. Just be a human checking in.
> · If they reply, reply back like a human. Don't turn their reply into a sales pitch.
