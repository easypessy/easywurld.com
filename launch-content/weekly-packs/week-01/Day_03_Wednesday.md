## 🕥 10:30 — LinkedIn Business 02 (Marketing insight)

**Headline:** The silent productivity killer in small businesses is not laziness. It is context switching between messaging, content and delivery.

> There is a cost that shows up in every small business I work with, and almost no founder measures it: the cost of switching contexts.
>
> A founder is writing a caption. A WhatsApp message comes in. They stop writing, reply to the message, try to remember what the caption was about, find the thought is gone, open Canva, get distracted by a delivery notification, deal with dispatch, and by the end of the morning no caption has been posted and three messages got half-replies.
>
> The cost of that switching is not just the five minutes per interruption. Studies on context switching place the recovery time at 23 minutes per interruption.
>
> AI does not solve this completely. But it reduces it in three practical ways:
>
> 1. Drafting support — a caption or a reply can be drafted while you are doing something else, so when you sit down to finish it you are not starting from zero.
> 2. Templated knowledge — if your prices, delivery terms and policies are written down once, AI can draft a reply from them instead of you retyping from memory every time.
> 3. Batch work — instead of answering messages one at a time all day, you can batch-reply from AI drafts at three set times.
>
> None of these require advanced tools. They require writing down the things you always say, and letting a tool do the first version.
>
> Productivity is not about working harder. It is about reducing the number of times a day you have to start over from a blank page or a blank chat box.
>

>

>
> - I still type a lot of messages completely by hand. The rule is not "AI for everything". It is "AI for the things I am tired of typing for the hundredth time".
