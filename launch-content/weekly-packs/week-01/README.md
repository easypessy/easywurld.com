## Carousel structure standard (§7)

- **Slide 1** — Hook. Stops the scroll. One big statement.
- **Slide 2** — Recognition. Makes the reader see their own problem.
- **Slides 3–5** — Development. Explain the problem, insight, or opportunity.
- **Slides 6–7** — Solution. Practical value, a checklist, a how-to.
- **Final slide** — Action. Save, share, comment, follow, or enquire. Logo lockup + slogan.

Not every carousel needs 7 slides. Use as many as the idea deserves (typically 5–7).

## Non-negotiable rules applied to every post

1. **One emotion per post** — relief, recognition, pride, honesty, belonging, anticipation or curiosity.
2. **Never invented proof** — no fake testimonials, fabricated results, or made-up client names. Demonstrations and concept projects are clearly labelled as such.
3. **One idea, three adaptations** — TikTok/IG/Facebook do not use the same caption.
4. **Short sentences, plain verbs, one terracotta accent.**
5. **Every CTA is specific** — save, share, comment with a word, WhatsApp a keyword, link in bio.
6. **End slide always** — EASYWURLD mark + "Simplify. Optimize. Grow." + one action.

## Production workflow reminder (§9)

**Sunday 8 November (batch day, ~90 minutes):**
1. Skim all seven days once.
2. Batch-design carousel slide backgrounds in Canva using the brand palette (cream bg, deep green serif headlines, one terracotta accent).
3. Schedule 07:45–13:45 posts in Meta Business Suite (Facebook + Instagram), TikTok app, LinkedIn.

5. Prepare story graphics for the next 2–3 days.

**Daily (5–10 minutes):**
- Check scheduled posts published.
- Post the 15:00, 16:30, 18:30, 20:30 slots manually if not scheduled.
- Post both stories (07:30, 12:30).
- Reply to every comment and DM before posting again.

**Sunday 15 November evening (review, ~15 minutes):**
- Record reach, saves, shares, comments, profile visits, DMs/enquiries, sales.
- Note the single best-performing post and the worst. Write one sentence to feed into Week 02.

This pack replaces the 37-post/day October phone plan with the new 41-post system described in the brief:
