## 🕣 08:45 — Post 02: Marketing lesson

**Core idea:** The first line of everything you write — first slide of a carousel, first line of a caption, first line of an ad, the subject line of an email — does 80% of the work. If it doesn't stop the scroll, the rest does not exist.
**Emotion:** Relief (a small fix with a big effect).
**Objective:** Education.

**Post text:**

>
> But no DMs. No WhatsApp messages. No one is picking up the phone.
>
> When I look at the posts, the reason is usually the same. The post has a point. It teaches something. It looks good. But at the end, there is no next step.
>
> Not a sales pitch. Just a specific action a person can take.
>
> "Save this for your next marketing review."
> "Drop a 1 in the comments if this is you."
> "Message the word CLARITY on WhatsApp if you want the workbook."
> "Tell me the last time a customer did this to you."
>
> People are obedient on social media. If you tell them what to do next, a surprising number will do it. If you do not tell them, they will like the post and scroll, because that is the easiest available action.
>
> A CTA does not have to be "buy now". It has to be a door.
>
> Likes do not pay staff. A clear next step is the difference between a post that performs and a post that produces something.
