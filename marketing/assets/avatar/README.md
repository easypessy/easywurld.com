No face is shown in any asset. No real person's likeness is used. All three stills are AI-generated scene art, not photographs of the owner.

## Rules

- Never crop or upscale a frame until eyes or a full face appear.
- Never add a name, age or biography caption.
- Every finished clip carries `AI presenter · Easywurld` and the platform AI label.
- Keep 20% negative space top and bottom for captions and the platform UI.

## Regenerate

Text prompts live in `marketing/AVATAR_OPERATOR.md`. Keep the palette (deep green `#0A4937`, leaf `#27473A`, cream `#FAE7C7`, terracotta accent), 35–50mm, shallow depth of field, 60–70% shadow.

Do not add these files to the carousel or meme builders; they are reference art for video production only.
