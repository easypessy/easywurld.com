# Starter asset kit

These are draft starter graphics, not finished videos and not published posts. Every CSV/app placement still starts Draft.

## Folders

- `series/`: 10 series cover cards. Use as series branding, not as a claim that every post is complete.
- `memes/`: 60 original meme PNGs, one for each daily meme slot.
- `launch-day/`: Monday 2 November fallback/carousel PNGs for all 10 core posts, plus four carousel ZIPs (S01, S06, S07, S10).
- `video/`: transparent AI-disclosure overlay and safe-area guide. No owner likeness or finished video.

## Important

- The Day 1 Trend Desk card says **HOLD**. Verify a current example and audio rights before publishing or recording.
- The AI and screen-demo slots are represented by fallback cards/real repo links; they are not finished videos.
- Confirm the offer price, payment and fulfilment before publishing any offer post.
- Confirm the owner likeness/voice permission before generating a personal AI presenter. Do not use the fictional demo founder.
- Regenerate with `/home/user/.venv/bin/python marketing/build-assets.py` after `npm run content:build`.
