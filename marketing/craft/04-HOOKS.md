# 04 — Hooks

*Seventy first lines, sorted by the mechanism that makes them work. Every one is ≤12 words, because
12 words is about two seconds of speech and two seconds is all you get.*

---

## 1. What a hook can and cannot do

**A hook buys you two seconds. Nothing else.** It cannot make a bad post good, it cannot sell, and
it cannot make you viral. Every honest source says the same thing: the first three seconds decide
whether the rest of the video is ever seen — and the rest of the video decides whether it travels.

A 2025 Meta study is widely cited as: **65% of people who watch the first 3 seconds stay for at
least 10 seconds, 45% for 30+ seconds.** *(Medium confidence — widely reported second-hand, I have
not read the study.)* The direction is what matters: the hook is a *gate*, not a *win*.

### The five things that make people stop

| # | Mechanism | Why it works | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Broken pattern** | The brain must classify anything that does not fit | Orienting response (**High**) |
| 2 | **A specific gap** | Curiosity is an itch, but only for a *specific*, *closeable* gap | Loewenstein, 1994 (**High**) |
| 3 | **Stakes for someone like them** | "This is about people like me" outranks "this is interesting" | Practitioner consensus (**Low**) |
| 4 | **A named thing they recognise** | Naming the villain is what earns the "this is me" comment | StoryBrand (**Medium**) |
| 5 | **A cost already paid** | Loss is felt harder than gain | Loss aversion (**High** as a principle, **Low** for any specific % here) |

### The three things that kill a hook

- **A greeting.** "Hey guys", "welcome back" — you have spent your two seconds saying nothing.
- **A question nobody needs answered.** "Do you want more customers?" The viewer answers yes and
  scrolls, because you told them nothing they did not know.
- **Vagueness dressed as drama.** "Something big is coming." There is no gap to close, so there is
  no itch, so there is no reason to stay.

## 3. The rewrite machine

You have a topic. You need five hooks in four minutes. Do this:

**Topic:** *"Shops should put the price on the post."*

| Step | Do this | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The fact** — say it plainly | "Most shops hide the price." |
| 2 | **The cost** — attach a consequence | "Hiding the price costs you the quiet customers." |
| 3 | **The contradiction** — say the opposite | "Showing the price is not what loses you customers." |
| 4 | **The witness** — steal a real sentence | "'I saw DM for price, so I left.'" |
| 5 | **The command** — make it doable | "Put the number in the first line. Today." |

Pick the one you would stop for. **Do not pick the cleverest one** — pick the one a tired shop
owner would understand in the first second.

### Watch the word count

The build fails on any hook over 12 words (`npm run audit:november`). Count before you fall in
love:

> 12 words ≈ 2–2.5 seconds spoken. That is the budget. Not a suggestion.

If a hook needs 20 words, it is not a hook — it is your first two sentences, and one of them has to
go.

## 4. What to do with a hook once posted

One number: **did they stay past 3 seconds?**

| Result | What it means | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Dies before 3s | The hook failed | Change the first frame. Not the script. |
| Lives past 3s, dies at 8s | The hook over-promised | Close the gap sooner, or make it more specific |
| Watched to the end, no shares | The hook was fine | The problem is the story, not the line |
| Saved a lot, low views | You made something useful for 40 people | That is not failure. That is a teach post doing its job. |

**Keep a file of the two hooks that worked each week.** In a month you will have eight proven
openers, and those are worth more than 70 untested ones — including these.

---

## 5. Hooks you must not use

You will see these constantly and they work in the short term:

- "Nobody is talking about this" — vague, and usually false.
- "Wait for it" — a tease that costs you the next video.
- "This one trick…" — the audience has been trained to scroll past it.
- "Watch before it gets deleted" — fake urgency, and the check in `npm run story` will fail it.
- Anything with a number you cannot show. If you say "40 people asked", you need the screenshot.
