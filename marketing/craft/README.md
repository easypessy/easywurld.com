# The craft library

This is where we keep the *how*. Not the plan, not the calendar — the mechanics.

You said: **"I will tell you about things, and from those things you construct."** That is exactly
how this folder works. Every time you see something that works, it comes here, gets taken apart,
and becomes a tool you can use on the next post or the next month.

## The loop

```
   you see something        →   it goes in OBSERVATIONS.md
   that works                     (what it was, where you saw it, date)
                                          │
                                          ▼
                              we take the MECHANIC out of it
                              (not the topic — the machine)
                                          │
                                          ▼
                              it becomes a numbered pattern
                              in 01 / 02 / 03, with templates
                                          │
                                          ▼
                              the next post or month is built
                              from the pattern, not from mood
```

**The rule that keeps this useful:** we take the **mechanic**, never the topic. A viral video about
a footballer teaches you nothing about football. It may teach you exactly how to hold a reveal for
eight seconds — and that works on a bakery page.

## How to add something (30 seconds, no ceremony)

Send it like this, or just talk and I will fill it in:

> **What I saw:** a child holding a bitten cardboard, seemed to be eating it
> **Where:** TikTok, this week
> **What happened at the end:** she pulled out the real thing she was eating
> **Why I think it worked:** you don't expect a child to eat cardboard, but you *do* expect a
> child to eat anything

Then I add: the mechanic, the timing, the business version, and the honesty limit. That last one
matters — some viral mechanics only work on entertainment, and forcing them onto a business post
makes the business look fake.

---

## Three rules for everything in this folder

1. **Nothing gets copied that we cannot do honestly.** If a mechanic needs a lie (a fake result,
   a fake customer, a fake emergency), it does not enter the library. Not because lying is rude —
   because one caught lie makes every real number in the account worthless.
2. **Every pattern must be testable in one week.** If we cannot tell in seven days whether it
   worked, it is a belief, not a tool.
3. **Patterns are not a personality.** The account is still "I read your page and tell you the
   truth." A pattern is how you say it, never what you are.

## Confidence labels used in every file

You asked for research, so the honesty has to be visible. Each claim is marked:

| Label | Means |
|---|---|
| **High** | Peer-reviewed or the platform's own documentation |
| **Medium** | Named study or benchmark, but reported second-hand |
| **Low** | A practitioner's blog or an agency's own numbers, with no method shown |
| **Reported** | You saw it, I did not. True as an observation, unverified as a fact |

Most short-form "data" on the internet is **Low**. It is still useful as a direction. It is not
useful as a promise, and I will not turn it into one.
