# Sources and verification notes

**Checked:** 29 October 2026
**Use:** These sources support the strategy, trend candidates, copywriting attributions and platform limits. Trend candidates still require a same-week check before publication. Platform limits can change; verify inside the account.

## Platform and AI rules

- TikTok AI-generated content labelling — realistic AI images, audio and video, including a person portrayed saying something they did not say; setting under **More options**. [1](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/creating-videos/ai-generated-content)
- TikTok Commercial Music Library — use for business content where available; personal-account trending sounds are not automatically cleared for brands. [2](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/how-to-use-the-commercial-music-library)
- TikTok video scheduler — official announcement describes scheduling from 15 minutes to 10 days ahead; scheduled posts cannot be edited, only deleted and re-uploaded. [1](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en/blog/introducing-video-scheduler-now-you-can-plan-tiktoks-in-advance)
- Meta Business Suite calendar — official guidance describes planning and scheduling Facebook and Instagram posts and stories from one calendar. Limits and features vary; check the account. [1](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/1170295510042639)
- TikTok creative guidance — hook early, human delivery, captions, vertical framing and a clear CTA. Creative principles, not reach guarantees. [1](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en/guides/what-is-ad-creative-guide)
- CapCut editing guidance — pair jump cuts with a meaningful reframe, B-roll, screen capture or caption change; cut around meaning. [1](https://www.capcut.com/create/video-transitions-crossfade-hard-cut-jump-cut)

## Copywriting attribution

The series paraphrases principles and uses **original Easywurld examples**. It does not reproduce long copyrighted advertisements.

- David Ogilvy — research, headlines, respect for the reader; *Ogilvy on Advertising*. [2](https://archive.org/details/ogilvyonadvertis0000ogil_g3n4)
- Eugene Schwartz — existing desire, awareness and market sophistication; *Breakthrough Advertising*. [1](https://www.npws.net/blog/key-takeaways-breakthrough-advertising)
- Joe Sugarman — reading momentum and the “slippery slide”; *The Adweek Copywriting Handbook*. [1](https://www.whiskeyandstardust.com/adweek-copywriting-handbook-book-notes)
- Gary Halbert — market/offer first and personal writing; *The Boron Letters*. [3](https://www.copyworkapp.com/gary-halbert-letters)
- Claude Hopkins — testing, specificity and reason-why; *Scientific Advertising*. [4](https://grokipedia.com/page/scientific_advertising_(book))
- John Caples — self-interest, news and curiosity in headlines; *Tested Advertising Methods*. [3](https://www.scribd.com/document/830542329/John-Caples-Tested-Advertising-Methods)
- Joanna Wiebe — voice-of-customer and conversion copy; Copyhackers. [1](https://copyhackers.com/about-us/) [2](https://copyhackers.com/join-the-conversation-customer/)
- Harry Dry — visual, falsifiable and unique copy; Marketing Examples. [1](https://marketingexamples.com/copywriting) [2](https://libraryofllm.com/sources/dry-marketing-examples)
- Ann Handley — utility, empathy and brand voice; *Everybody Writes*. [2](https://www.supersummary.com/everybody-writes/summary/)
- Robert Bly — headline functions, benefits and clear CTA; *The Copywriter’s Handbook*. [1](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/162340.The_Copywriter_s_Handbook)

## Claims policy used in the plan

- No invented client results, testimonials, rankings, revenue, follower counts or conversion lifts.
- No claim that a format guarantees reach or sales.
- No claim that the plan has been scheduled or published.
- No claim that all trends are current until the verification log is complete.
- No claim that every AI output is accurate; human review is mandatory.
