## 7. From Sunday 1 Nov — the daily rhythm

Two blocks. Set two alarms. That's the whole discipline.

**9:00–9:45am — 10 messages.** Use Message 1 from `MESSAGES.txt`. Change the bracketed part
every single time. Log each one in `targets.csv`: sent / replied / bought.

**7:00–7:45pm — 10 messages, then post 3 times.** Same as the morning. Then:
1. one **teardown** ("here is what's wrong with this page")
2. one **checklist** ("three things to fix today")
3. one **honest ad** ("this is what I sell, this is what it costs")

Film all 21 of the week's videos on Sunday if you can. If you can't, film 3 in the morning
before the messages. **The messages come first. Always.** Posts build the audience; messages
pay for data this month.

**Three per day is the deal, not eleven.** The calendar has 11 slots a day because it was
built to be generous. Use three. A month of three beats four days of eleven.

## 8. The only three numbers

Every Friday, write these down. Nothing else matters yet.

| Number | How | Bad | Fine | Good |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Reply rate** | replies ÷ messages sent | 5% | 10% | 20% |
| **Close rate** | sales ÷ replies | 10% | 15% | 20% |
| **Shares per post** | Instagram insights | 0 | 1–3 | 5+ |

Watch **shares**, not likes. A share means someone thought "this is my friend's shop". That is
the only signal that predicts growth for an account nobody knows.

## 9. Ignore all of this

- **Any video about going viral.** You are not going viral this month. You are getting paid.
- **Editing, transitions, aesthetics.** Nobody has ever bought a page fix because of a transition.
- **Buying followers, follow-for-follow, engagement pods.** They poison the account.
- **"Best time to post."** Worth about 2%. Your calendar already spreads the times.
- **The 990-piece schedule.** It is done. You do not need to look at it again except to film.
- **Building anything new.** Not one thing. Not a link-in-bio, not a landing page, not a logo.

## 10. If nothing is working by day 14 (Sat 14 Nov)

Look at your real reply rate and pick one:

| What you see | What it means | What to change |
|---|---|---|
| Under 5% replies | The message is the problem, not the market | Send 20 by hand, one sentence each, mentioning a real thing you saw. No template. |
| 10%+ replies, no sales | The offer is the problem | Drop the pack, sell the ₦5,000 review. Simpler to say yes to. |
| Replies and sales, low post shares | Your posts aren't the reason — the DMs are | Keep DMs, post once a day, stop worrying about content |
| Everything dead | ₦5,000 may be the wrong price for a stranger | Try ₦3,000 for the first five, then go back up |

**Do not change the plan before day 14.** Changing it every three days is why most people's
first month produces nothing but screenshots.
