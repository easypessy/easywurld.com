## 1. The honest read

### 1.1 The volume math

| Unit | Per day | Per 30 days | Per 90 days |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ideas | 11 | 330 | 990 |
| Platform posts (×3) | 33 | 990 | 2,970 |
| Carousels (Instagram backbone) | ~4 | 120 | 360 |
| Short videos (TikTok backbone) | ~11 | 330 | 990 |
| Written memes | 3 | 90 | 270 |
| Skits | 1 | 30 | 90 |

That is the plan you asked for, and it is achievable — **but only with templates and batching.** Built ad hoc, each day costs 5–6 hours and collapses by week three. Built properly, it costs about 3 hours a day, plus one long Sunday.

### 1.2 Where Month 1 money actually comes from

This is the part most content plans get wrong, so it is stated plainly:

**Platform payouts are not Month 1 money. In Nigeria, on TikTok, they are not any money.**

- **TikTok Creator Rewards is not available in Nigeria.** The programme runs in eight markets (US, UK, France, Germany, Japan, South Korea, Brazil, Mexico). Nigerian views earn ₦0 from it regardless of who watches. Nigerian creators monetise through brand deals, LIVE gifts, affiliate and — the only one that matters here — **selling their own product**.
- **Facebook is monetisable in Nigeria**, but the on-demand in-stream bar is 5,000 followers plus 60,000 minutes viewed in 60 days, and boosted or paid views do not count. That is a Month 4–6 possibility, not a Month 1 one.
- **Instagram pays nothing directly** at this scale.

So the Month 1 revenue line is: **packs (₦5,000) sold through DMs, plus the first project (₦150,000).** Everything in this plan is built to produce those two things.

### 1.3 The three levers, in order of power

| Lever | What it does | Month 1 weight |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Outbound free site reviews** | 10 short voice notes a week to Lagos businesses. No pitch. This is the highest-converting hour you will spend. | 55% of revenue |
| **2. Daily content** | Builds proof, familiarity and inbound DMs. Compounds slowly, then fast. | 30% of revenue |
| **3. DMs on every comment, save and reply** | Turns attention into conversations. Where the pack closes. | 15% of revenue, growing |

**The uncomfortable truth:** 33 posts a day to an audience of zero produces very few views in week one. If Month 1 is left to content alone, it will not hit its number. Outbound is not optional — it is the engine, and content makes it credible.

| 6 | 13:45 | **Teach** | Authority | TikTok · IG carousel · FB |

| 8 | 16:30 | **Meme** | Reach | TikTok · IG meme · FB |
| 9 | 18:00 | **Upgrade** | Points at the ₦150k+ project | TikTok · IG carousel · FB |

**Mix: Teach 3 · Meme 3 · Offer 2 · Trust 1 · Hot take 1 · Skit 1 · Upgrade 1 = 11.**

Memes are 27% of output — inside the 20–30% band that keeps a feed human instead of a billboard.

**Change from v1:** LinkedIn drops out of the daily count. It is a 4th platform, and it was quietly making the day 44 posts. Run it only on the two Upgrade posts a week, from the personal profile, if the time exists.

### 3.2 The production model — one pass, thirty-three outputs

The mistake is treating 33 posts as 33 creations. It is **11 assets, each rendered three ways.**

```
ONE IDEA
  ├─ TikTok: video (kinetic type / voiceover / skit)
  ├─ Instagram: carousel or meme (5 slides, same copy broken up)
  └─ Facebook: 120-word text post (same argument, written for readers)
```

Same idea, three shapes. That is why the day files in Drive work — and why the bottleneck is not writing, it is **rendering**.

### 3.3 The weekly production budget

| Session | When | What gets made | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Batch A — shoot** | Sunday | 7 skits (one location, one wardrobe change), 11 voiceovers recorded, 21 meme backgrounds exported | 4h |
| **Batch B — build** | Wednesday | 28 carousels (4/day × 7) from 5 master templates, all text set | 3h |
| **Daily assembly** | Every day | Cut 11 videos in CapCut from templates, adapt 33 captions, schedule | 90 min |
| **Daily selling** | Every day | 2 free reviews, DM replies, follow-ups, tracker | 45 min |
| **Weekly** | Friday | Scorecard, kill/keep decisions, next week's hooks | 30 min |
| **Total** | | | **~22h/week** |

That is the real number. Not 5 hours. Twenty-two. It fits a serious side business or a full-time operator; it does not fit around a full-time job unless you use the fallback below.

### 3.4 Three cadence modes (so the system survives real life)

| Mode | Posts/day | When | What it contains |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Full** | 33 | Default weeks | All 11 slots, all 3 platforms |
| **Standard** | 18 | Busy week, travel, low energy | 6 ideas: 1 meme, 2 teach, 1 offer, 1 upgrade, 1 skit |
| **Minimum** | 4 | Sick, travelling, emergency | 1 meme, 1 teach carousel, 1 offer, 1 upgrade |

**The rule: never zero.** A Minimum Day keeps the algorithm warm and the habit alive. Do not "make up" missed posts the next day — the feed is not a debt ledger.

### 3.5 The template library (build this first)

Before day one, build these once. Everything after is assembly.

- **5 carousel templates** (hook / list / before-after / quote / offer) — 1080×1350
- **4 video templates** (kinetic type, voiceover+B-roll, skit, hot take) — 1080×1920
- **3 meme templates** (two-panel, chat screenshot, statement card) — 1080×1350
- **1 caption bank** per platform, with the CTA block pre-written
- **1 end card** (mark + EASYWURLD + slogan)

Template build time: about two days. It pays back inside the first week.

## 4. The nine offers — separated, tiered, standalone

### 4.1 The standalone rule

Every offer must satisfy all four:

1. Its own pack document, self-contained.
2. Its own 12 posts, written for that offer alone.
3. Its own keyword (PLAYBOOK, CLEAR, ENGINE…).
4. **No post depends on a post about a different offer.** Pull one offer out for a week and the feed still makes sense.

Drive already satisfies this: each of the nine folders has THE PACK, SALES SHEET, STANDALONE POSTS, CAROUSELS, VOICEOVERS, MEMES, UPGRADE PATH. Do not bundle the folders together when selling or when briefing.

### 4.2 The tiering — what gets the slots

Nine offers at equal weight means nine half-launches. **Weight the offer slots, keep every pack sellable.**

| Tier | Offers | Share of offer slots | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A — Flagship (3)** | PLAYBOOK · CLEAR · PAGE | 60% | Painful, immediate, cheap to fix, and each one upgrades naturally into a ₦150k build |
| **B — Supporting (4)** | ENGINE · LOCAL · LAUNCH · PROMPTS | 30% | Strong, but the buyer needs to already believe they have the problem |
| **C — Specialist (2)** | MESSAGE · TEMPLATES | 10% | B2B-professional buyers, lower volume, high retention |

**Which project each flagship points at:**

- **PLAYBOOK** → WhatsApp business system / landing page with a chat flow
- **CLEAR** → Clear Homepage project (₦150k landing page)
- **PAGE** → One-Offer Landing Page project (₦150k)

### 4.3 The ascension that does not break the rule

The nine stay separate. But a buyer who has bought two packs should be offered the **Studio Bundle — all nine for ₦25,000** as an upgrade, not as the entry price. This does three things: it preserves the ₦5,000 entry, it raises average order value, and it makes the project conversation easier ("you already have the system — want it built?").

### 4.4 The rotation

One week = one flagship offer + one supporting offer. Nine offers cycle over roughly five weeks, with the flagships repeating twice as often. This keeps the feed coherent for a week at a time, which is also what makes a buyer move.

## 5. The 3–6 month growth plan

### 5.1 Why skits, and only skits, carry the virality target

Published 2026 Nigerian TikTok medians:

| Format | Median views |
|---|---|
| Skit | ~148,000 |
| Voiceover + B-roll | ~17,000 |
| Talking head | ~3,200 |

A skit reaches roughly 9× a voiceover and 46× a talking head. Teaching builds authority; **skits are the only format that breaks you out of your existing followers.** One a day is the floor. Batch-shoot seven every Sunday.

**No face required.** Hands, objects, WhatsApp screens, a phone face-down on a table, voiceover over a scene. The joke is the mechanism, not the face. If you are camera-shy, the system still works.

### 5.2 The hook library (the real growth asset)

Every post lives or dies in three seconds. Build a bank of 100 hooks, tagged by format, and test them:

- **Contrarian:** "Your price is not why they disappeared."
- **Specific number:** "Three lines. That's the whole reply."
- **Callout:** "Lagos business owners, this one is for you."
- **Before/after:** "This homepage said nothing. Watch."
- **Confession:** "I sent this exact message for two years and lost sales."
- **Curiosity gap:** "The one thing every ₦5,000 pack gets wrong."

Rotate 7 hooks a week. Track which hook drove the highest 3-second retention. **Kill the bottom third every month.**

### 5.3 The swing system

Volume alone does not go viral; tested angles do. Twice a week, post one deliberate **swing** — a format or angle outside the normal rotation. Review it after 72 hours on one number: **views vs your 30-day median.**

- Above 3× median → make three more of it this month.
- Below 0.5× median → retire the angle, not the format.
- In between → leave it in rotation.

This is how a feed discovers its own winning format instead of hoping.

### 5.4 Milestones

| Day | Followers (combined) | View milestone | Business milestone |
|---|---|---|---|
| 30 | 800–1,500 | 3 videos > 10k · 1 > 50k | 12 packs · 1 project · 1 care |
| 60 | 3,000–5,000 | 2 videos > 100k | 25 packs · 3 projects total |
| 90 | 10,000–15,000 | 1 video > 500k | ₦1m+ cumulative · care base ₦100k/mo |
| 120 | 20,000–30,000 | median views doubled | first brand-deal conversation |
| 180 | 40,000–60,000 | 2 videos > 1m | Facebook monetisation application window · paid community open |

**Measure median, not best.** A single viral video with a flat rest-of-feed is luck. A rising median is a business.

### 5.5 What "viral with money" requires

Viral views only become revenue if three doors are open before the spike:

1. **Profile** — one line that says what you do, who for, and what to do next.
2. **One link** — a link-in-bio page listing the nine packs, the project menu and WhatsApp. Not a homepage with nine tabs.
3. **A DM keyword** — every video says a word ("PLAYBOOK"), and every DM gets a saved reply within 15 minutes.

A 500k-video with no door is a nice week and an empty account.

## 6. THE ASSET LAW — safe zones, in all three senses

Every carousel, meme, video and text post passes this section before it ships. No exceptions, because inconsistency is what makes a feed look amateur at volume.

### 6.1 Visual safe zone — the one-frame rule

Design every vertical video on a **1080 × 1920** canvas, and keep **all text, faces and the logo inside one box:**

> **x: 90 → 990 · y: 270 → 1250**  (a 900 × 980 box)

That single box is safe on TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels and Stories simultaneously. If it fits there, it fits everywhere. Everything outside it is where the platform puts its own buttons.

**Per-platform detail, for reference:**

| Placement | Size | Top dead zone | Bottom dead zone | Side margins |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok (video) | 1080×1920 | 108–130 px | 320 px | 60 left · 120 right |
| Instagram Reels | 1080×1920 | 270 px (14%) | 672 px (35%) | 65 px |
| Facebook Reels | 1080×1920 | 100 px | 300 px | 60 px |
| Stories (IG/FB) | 1080×1920 | 250 px | 340 px | 54 px |
| IG/FB feed portrait | 1080×1350 | 100 px | 180 px | 100 px |
| Feed square (WhatsApp/X) | 1080×1080 | 90 px | 90 px | 90 px |

**Carousel rules:**
- 1080×1350 (4:5), 5–10 slides, PNG.
- 100 px margin minimum on all sides; 180 px at the bottom on slide 1 if it will be seen in Reels.
- One idea per slide, headline 5–8 words.
- Swipe cue on slides 1 to n−1; CTA and lockup on the last.
- The mark sits bottom-right with clear space — never under text, never stretched.

**Meme rules:**
- 1080×1350 for IG/FB, 1080×1080 for WhatsApp/status.
- 8% margins (≈86 px / 108 px) all round.
- Cream `#FAE7C7`, deep green `#0A4937`, **one** terracotta `#9E4E2C` accent.
- No logo on the meme. No pitch on the meme.
- Three checks: would a customer find it funny · is it a punch down · is the joke clear in two seconds.

**Video rules:**
- 15–30 seconds; hook in 3; jump cut every 3–5; text on every frame.
- Captions burned in — most sessions are silent.
- Text in the top half of the safe box, never bottom third.
- End card: mark + EASYWURLD + slogan, held 2 seconds.

### 6.2 Algorithmic safe zone — what gets you suppressed

This is the part nobody writes down, and it is the difference between 33 posts a day working and 33 posts a day being throttled.

| Never | Why |
|---|---|
| Visible TikTok watermark on a cross-post | Both platforms demote recycled content |
| Reposting someone else's video unedited | Not original; demonetised and down-ranked |
| Bought followers or engagement pods | Kills reach and blocks monetisation later |
| "Comment YES to get the link" engagement bait | Explicitly demoted by Meta and TikTok |
| Guaranteed-income or guaranteed-results claims | Policy breach and consumer-protection risk |
| Medical, financial or legal advice dressed as fact | Needs credentials you do not have |
| Music from the commercial library on a business account | Business accounts lose most trending sounds — use original audio/voiceover |
| Text in the bottom 30% | Covered by the UI; reads as a design error |
| The same caption pasted to all three platforms | Reads as spam, and each platform's audience behaves differently |
| More than one terracotta accent per asset | Breaks the brand, looks like clip-art |

**Account type matters.** If TikTok Creator Rewards ever opens to Nigeria, it requires a **personal** account — business accounts are excluded, and business accounts also lose most of the sound library. Recommend: **personal/creator account on TikTok**, business accounts on Facebook and Instagram.

### 6.3 The Safe Space — the product, and its rule in the content

The Safe Space is two things, and they are sequenced, not simultaneous:

1. **The community** — a free WhatsApp Channel first, then a paid WhatsApp/Telegram group (₦2,000–₦15,000/month) once the channel passes 50 subscribers. Not before. A paid group with no audience is unpaid admin work.
2. **The studio** — a place to shoot skits and produce content. Month 4–6, only if the group has paying members.

**The content rule:** **one post a week** gestures at the space — a taste of what members get, never a pitch. It is slot 8 on Saturdays (the meme slot), because the community promise is belonging, not instruction.

**How it appears inside other assets:** the carousel's final slide may carry the line *"Members get the templates first — link in bio."* That is the whole integration. The Safe Space never takes an offer slot until Month 3.

**Honest note carried forward from v1:** the original survey behind the Solution Center had three respondents. That is a signal, not a market. Build the cheap version, let paying people tell you what they want.

### 6.4 The App

Post Commander (in `03 — CONTENT ENGINE`) is the phone-side interface: open today's date, see all 33 posts, copy, build, post, tick off.

For the app to be usable, every asset must be **named and dated to match the day file** — `D03-07_UPGRADE_TIKTOK.mp4`, `D03-07_UPGRADE_IG_5slide.png`. Assets stored loose in a camera roll will kill the system by week two.

### 6.5 The pre-publish checklist — 10 checks

1. Text inside the safe box (x 90–990, y 270–1250 for video)
2. One terracotta accent maximum
3. Copy spelled exactly, no placeholder text
4. No watermark, no other brand's logo
5. Captions burned in (video) / swipe cue present (carousel)
6. Caption written for *this* platform, not pasted
7. One clear next step — keyword, link or save
8. No guaranteed-results or invented-proof claim
9. Hook lands in the first 3 seconds / first line
10. Named to the day-and-slot convention

## 7. The high-ticket door

The offers pay the bills. **Projects pay for the year.** They do not come from pack buyers at volume — they come from a deliberate weekly action.

### 7.1 The path

```
FREE SITE REVIEW (90-sec voice note, 3 fixes, no pitch)
        ↓  ~30% reply
CONVERSATION (WhatsApp, human, useful)
        ↓  ~35% call
AUDIT CALL (20 min, screen shared, 3 problems named)
        ↓  ~35% proposal
PROPOSAL (fixed price, fixed scope, 5-day validity)
        ↓  ~25% close
PROJECT (₦150k–₦650k)  →  CARE (₦25k/mo)
```

**40 reviews → 12 replies → 4 calls → 1–2 projects.** That is the Month 1 path to the ₦150k.

### 7.2 Weekly targets

| Action | Per week | Per month |
|---|---|---|
| Free site reviews sent | 10 | 40 |
| Replies handled | 3–5 | 12–20 |
| Audit calls | 1–2 | 4–8 |
| Proposals sent | 1 | 4 |
| Projects closed | — | 1–2 |

### 7.3 The assets this door needs (and does not have yet)

- **Audit template** — one page, three problems, three fixes, one price.
- **Proposal template** — scope, price, timeline, what is not included, payment terms, 5-day validity.
- **Three case studies** — real builds, labelled *concept project · sample build* where they are not client work. Never invent proof.
- **A link-in-bio page** — nine packs, project menu, WhatsApp. One link.
- **A saved-replies library** — the nine keywords, price question, timeline question, "too expensive".

## 9. Metrics — six numbers, once a week

| # | Metric | Where | Healthy month 1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **DMs started** | Inbox count | 60+ |
| 2 | **Conversations → pack sales** | Tracker | 10–15% |
| 3 | **Median views per video** | TikTok/IG analytics | rising week on week |
| 4 | **Saves + shares** (IG) | Insights | 2%+ of reach |
| 5 | **Reviews sent → replies** | Your own sheet | 25–35% |
| 6 | **Cash collected** | Bank/Paystack | vs the §2.3 floor |

**Likes are not a metric.** They are a by-product. The only three that decide whether this plan continues are **DMs, cash, and median views.**

### The weekly scorecard (Friday, 30 minutes)

## 10. Compliance and platform safety

*Not legal advice — a checklist to take to a Nigerian accountant or lawyer once.*

**Business basics**

- **CAC registration** — register the business name before invoicing projects. Corporate clients will ask.
- **TIN** — required to open a business bank account and to invoice properly.
- **VAT** — Nigerian VAT registration applies above the small-company turnover threshold (₦25m under current rules). Below it, you generally do not charge VAT — confirm this with your accountant, and keep records either way.
- **Separate account** — business payments must not land in a personal account once projects start.

**Selling digital products**

- Publish **what the buyer gets**, delivery method, and support terms on every sales sheet.
- Do **not** write "no refunds, no exceptions." Nigerian consumer protection requires fair terms. Use: *"Digital download. If the file is faulty or will not open, we replace it within 7 days. Results are not guaranteed."*
- **No income or results guarantees** anywhere in the copy. The existing pack language already does this well — keep it.
- Label every non-client build **concept project · sample build**. Never imply a client relationship that does not exist.

**Data**

- **NDPA 2023** — get opt-in before adding anyone to a WhatsApp broadcast list or email list. A "message me your number" is consent to reply, not consent to market.
- Publish a one-page privacy note: what you collect, why, how to be removed.
- Delete prospect lists on request, without argument.

**Platforms**

- **Disclose AI-generated content** where the platform requires it. Text-in-image and synthetic voice are the usual triggers.
- **No bought followers, no engagement pods, no follow-for-follow.** These are the fastest way to lose monetisation eligibility before you ever get it.
- **Original audio only** on business accounts — the commercial library is restricted.
- Keep a **backup of every asset** outside the phone. Accounts get restricted; the content should survive it.
