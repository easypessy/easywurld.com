## Daily research intake

1. Collect audience questions from consented conversations, on-site FAQs, permissioned support patterns and actual analytics. Do not copy private messages into an AI prompt. Record date, origin, sample size/selection bias and what cannot be generalized.
2. Keep a queue tagged to: acquisition/retention, social strategy, content, copy/psychology, web conversion, bounded AI/automation, niche opportunities and platform changes. For a proposed claim, first seek official platform docs, source research or a credible firsthand publication; note its date and scope. For expert advice, attribute only after reading the original context; create a new Easywurld interpretation, not a copied article.
3. Ledger fields: claim ID, exact wording and implied claim, type (fact/opinion/hypothesis/illustrative), source URL or local path, retrieved date, publisher, method/scope, confidence/limitations, rights, approved qualifier, reviewer and review expiry. Empty source for a factual claim = G1 hold. Do not use a stat without a traceable original and method.
4. Prepare a brief: real business question → evidence → counterexample/limit → audience consequence → proposed test → success measure. Label hypothetical examples on the first slide and caption.
5. Before each G4, check fresh platform policy, current offer terms, rights, destination, no queue collision and screenshot/asset integrity. Record actual account confirmation, not a remembered platform limit.
