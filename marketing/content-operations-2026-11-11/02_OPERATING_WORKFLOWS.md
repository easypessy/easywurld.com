# Daily department runbook — 11–30 November

## Cadence and editorial allocation

The owner confirmed the **period** 11–30 November; the new request supplies **12 master carousels/day**, **three supporting stories/day**, **five company LinkedIn posts/day**, **three personal LinkedIn posts/day**. Do not silently downgrade this target, and do not mistake it for 12 × 3 different creative projects: the 12 are adapted to Instagram, Facebook and TikTok photo. This is a production **target**, not an order to publish weak work. If quality, capacity or approvals fail, report the shortfall and hold the slot. No LinkedIn post is forced into carousel format; text is used for the pilot.

Eleven permanent series receive a daily candidate. The twelfth is selected from the series with a genuine additional audience problem and distinct objective. For 11 Nov, a second diagnosis tests delivery-area uncertainty rather than another offer pitch. Reach (S01/S12), education (S02/S08), trust (S03/S04/S06), demonstration (S05/S07/S09), awareness (S10/S11) and conversion (S07/S10) are represented; this is a strategic map, not measured effectiveness. On subsequent dates select the second slot from research, not a mechanical rotation.

## Story and LinkedIn editorial workflows

- **Story 1** asks a question (poll/sticker if supported); capture consented themes, never a customer name. **Story 2** applies a technique from that day's feed, not a duplicate slide. **Story 3** invites a useful enquiry only after service/inbox availability is confirmed. Each has exact copy, visual, interaction and CTA in the pilot.
- Company LinkedIn: 5 independent text-led drafts per day with business relevance, analysis/demonstration and a non-forced CTA; use research source IDs and accurate company voice. Personal LinkedIn: 3 first-person **proposals** per day, reviewed by the actual account owner; never manufacture experiences, metrics, clients or qualifications. If account access is missing, export the copy and leave status pending.

## Failed-content revision loop

Reviewer marks issue and severity → named writer records revision ID and changed claim/slides/asset → researcher checks evidence and permissions → designer re-exports → new phone/platform check and re-score → named approver records G4 decision. Old version remains in Git history or approved version store; never silently alter a queued post. On a rights/privacy/factual failure, stop even if arithmetic reaches 85.
