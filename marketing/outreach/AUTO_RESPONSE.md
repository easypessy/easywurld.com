# What happens when they respond: the three-layer inbox

Yes — the first response is automatic. The **selling** is not. This is the same
line we sell: the drafting tool and the auto-reply do the instant work, a person
does the thinking. If our own inbox auto-negotiates, the human-check promise is a
lie.

Layer timings are the standard. Anything slower than this loses the chat, because
the buyer messages three vendors at once and buys from whoever answered clearly
first.

| Layer | What it is | Speed | Who |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0** | Automatic acknowledgment + one qualifying question + the price boundary | instant, 24/7 | WhatsApp Business greeting/away, IG saved auto-replies |
| **1** | Human answer with their business in it: the rewrite, the pack that fits, the payment route | ≤ 3 working hours (≤ 1 hour 08:00-20:00) | you |
| **2** | Custom demo for **their** business, or a scoped custom quote | ≤ 1 working day | you |

### Instagram / Facebook
- **Saved replies**: same nine texts (IG has no `/` shortcuts — save them as
  `price`, `packs`, `demo`, `pay`, `deliver`, `follow1`, `custom`, `no`, `ai`).
- **Frequently asked questions** (IG professional dashboard → auto-replies): one
  question "How much?" → the `/price` text. That is the only auto-answer we run on
  IG, because "how much" is 8 out of 10 first messages.
- Comments asking price: reply publicly with "Sent you a DM" and DM within the
  hour. Never leave "DM for price" as the answer — that phrase alone makes buyers
  with money pass.

### LinkedIn (company page + personal)
No auto-reply exists for a Page, so Layer 0 is a **notification routine** instead:
- Phone notifications on for the Page; check at 08:00, 12:00, 17:00.
- Comment replies within 3 working hours; every link goes in the first comment,
  never in the post body.
- First saved snippet: the `/demo` text, adapted to "your business" → their
  company name.

## Triage: what their message means and what fires

| They say | Layer 0 fires | Layer 1 human does |
|---|---|---|
| "How much?" / "price?" / "how much last" | `/price` | name their business, offer the free one-line rewrite, ask which pack |
| "DM for price" reply to our post | greeting | `/demo` — rewrite one of their lines, free |
| "Can you do it for me?" / "build my site" | greeting | `/custom` — scoped quote in one day, never inside the ₦5,000 |
| "I will get back to you" | — | label **Not now**, start 1-3-7 (day 1, day 3, day 7), then stop |
| "Is this real / are you a scam?" | — | human, within the hour: business name, what the files are, that nothing is hosted or auto-sent, and payment on their terms. Never argue. |
| "I don't have a website" | — | re-tier: PLAYBOOK or LOCAL first. Do not sell CLEAR to someone with no site. |
| Complaint or refund | — | human within 1 hour. No auto-reply on a complaint, ever. |
| "Is this AI?" | `/ai` | honest, short, then back to their problem |

**Never automatic:** a price change, a custom scope, a promise about results, a
refund decision, or anything that mentions their numbers. Those are Layer 1-2
human work — and that boundary is what we sell.

## Labels and the follow-up clock (WhatsApp Business labels)

`New` → `Tier 1` / `Tier 2` / `Tier 3` → `Rewrite sent` → `Quote sent` →
`Paid` → `Delivered` → `Not now` (revisit in 90 days) → `Closed`

The 1-3-7 rhythm: day 1 = the value message (`/follow1`), day 3 = one specific
thing they can fix today with no purchase, day 7 = last note plus `/no` as the
easy exit. Three touches, then the lead rests. A fourth message reads as pressure
and costs the brand more than the sale is worth.

## What we count (weekly, 15 minutes)

- First reply within 5 minutes: share of chats — target ≥ 90% while Layer 0 is on.
- Chat → rewrite sent → reply: how many free rewrites earn an answer.
- Rewrite → paid: the conversion we actually sell.
- Follow-up recoveries: sales that came from day 3 or day 7, not day 1.
- Where chats stall: greeting, price, quote, payment. Fix that step only.

Log the four numbers in `marketing/MEASUREMENT.csv` each Friday.
