EASYWURLD — THE THREE MESSAGES
Copy these into WhatsApp > Settings > Quick Replies.
Replace everything in [brackets]. Never send one that could have gone to anybody else.

================================================================
MESSAGE 1 — THE FINDING (this is the one you send first)
================================================================

Good afternoon [name]. I was looking at [business] on Google and noticed
[one specific thing: the listing says closed / there is no price anywhere /
the number goes to a different business]. I fix pages for small Lagos
businesses, so I wrote down the three things I would change — want me to
send them? No charge, no pitch.

----------------------------------------------------------------
Fill-ins that actually work (be this specific, or don't send it):

  "your Google listing says CLOSED but your Instagram says you're open"
  "there's no price anywhere on the page, so people have to ask"
  "your bio says what you do in a way I couldn't understand in 4 seconds"
  "the last 3 posts have no caption, just photos"
  "your pinned post is from 2024"
  "the WhatsApp link in your bio doesn't open"
  "you get asked 'how much' in the comments, every time"

================================================================
MESSAGE 2 — THE THREE POINTS (only if they say yes / "ok send")
================================================================

Here they are, in order of what is costing you most:

1. [specific fix]
2. [specific fix]
3. [specific fix]

That is the free version and it is yours to keep. If you want it done
properly: I read your page and your last twenty chats and send back the
three fixes in order, plus the exact words to copy. ₦5,000, one page,
two days. Call it The Silence Fix.

Message SILENCE and I will send three questions. Pay after you have seen
the first fix if you prefer — I would rather you judged the work than my
profile. Free sample of how I write, attached.

================================================================
MESSAGE 3 — THE FOLLOW-UP (day 3, ONCE, then leave them alone)
================================================================

Following up once, then I'll leave you alone. The offer is ₦5,000 and it
won't bring you new customers — it stops you losing the ones already
messaging you. If now isn't the time, say so and I'll check back in January.

================================================================
WHEN THEY SAY THESE THINGS
================================================================

"how much?"
  → "₦5,000. That is the whole price and there is only one thing I sell:
     I read your page and your last twenty chats, and send back three
     fixes in order plus the exact words to copy. Two days."

"send account"
  → Send your bank details with your NAME as the account name, and say:
     "Send the alert screenshot here and I'll start within the hour."

"is this real?"
  → "Fair question. Here's my page [link], here's the free sample [PDF],
     and my number is the same one on my profile. Pay after you see the
     first fix if you prefer — I would rather you judged the work."

"later / I'll get back to you"
  → "No problem. I'll check back in January." Then actually do that.
     Do not message again this month.

"no reply ever"
  → One follow-up on day 3 (Message 3). Then nothing. Ever.

================================================================
RULES THAT KEEP YOU HONEST
================================================================
- ONE offer only: The Silence Fix, ₦5,000. Never list the nine packs
  to a stranger. If they ask what else you do: "One thing, for now."
- Never say "guaranteed", "viral", or promise followers or sales.
- Never invent a client, a result, or a number.
- Never say you looked at something you didn't look at.
- If you didn't check the page, don't send the message.
