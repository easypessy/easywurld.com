# Finding the leads: 20 minutes a day, by tier

**Honest limit first.** I cannot hand you a list of real phone numbers: I have no
access to a business directory, a WhatsApp index or your contacts, and inventing
names and numbers would send you chasing people who do not exist. What this gives
you is the exact routine that fills `LEAD_SHEET.csv` with real, reachable contacts
— 20 minutes a day, 8-12 leads, ranked by tier before you send anything.

Everything below is searchable from a phone. Record the handle, the city, the
channel and the **one line you will rewrite** — that line is what makes the
message land.

---

## Start here: Tier 0, the people you already have (30 minutes, once)

The warmest leads are not on any list. Open, in this order:

1. **Your WhatsApp** → archived and unread chats, plus every "how much?" that
   never got a clear answer. Those are people who already raised a hand.
2. **Instagram / Facebook DMs** → unanswered and answered-but-unsold.
3. **Your phone contacts** → anyone running a business: the tailor, the caterer,
   the salon, the school, the photographer, the cousin with the wig shop.
4. **Past enquiries on the Easywurld site and posts** → everyone who commented
   "price?" or "interested".

Write them into `LEAD_SHEET.csv` as Tier 0. Ten of these beat a hundred cold
contacts.

---

## Tier 1.1 — chat sellers with "DM for price"

**Where:** Instagram and Facebook, then WhatsApp status.

- Instagram search: your city + trade hashtags — `#adoekitivendors`,
  `#ekitibusiness`, `#lagosvendors`, `#naijafashion`, `#asoebivendor`,
  `#foodvendorlagos`, `#wigvendor`, `#phoneaccessories`.
- In the results, open the recent tab and look for **these visible signals**:
  - bio or caption contains "DM for price", "DM to order", "inbox me"
  - no price anywhere on the page, no link, no WhatsApp button
  - posts daily but comments ask "how much?" repeatedly with no reply, or replies
    that say "check your DM"
  - a WhatsApp link that goes to a chat with no greeting message
- Facebook: groups such as *Nigerian Small Business Owners*, *Lagos Vendors*,
  *Buy and Sell [your city]* — sort by newest, look for posts asking how to get
  more customers or complaining about "serious buyers".

**What you record:** handle, city, what they sell, the exact line you will rewrite
(their bio line or their "DM for price"), whether they have a WhatsApp button.

## Tier 1.2 — websites that fail the ten-second test

**Where:** Google.

- Search: `"[trade] in [city]"` — e.g. `interior decorator in Ado-Ekiti`,
  `catering services in Ikeja`, `school in Ibadan`. Open the results that are
  **their own website** (not Jumia, not a directory, not Instagram).
- Score each in ten seconds: does the first line say what they sell, who it is
  for, and what to do next? Headlines to look for: "Welcome to our website",
  "We are a leading provider of quality services", "Home — [Business Name]".
- Also check `site:[theirdomain]` — one page and nothing else means no service
  pages, which is Tier 2.1 as well.

**What you record:** the URL, the headline exactly as written, your rewrite.

## Tier 1.3 — one offer, nowhere to send people

**Where:** Instagram and LinkedIn.

- Look for a business posting the **same offer repeatedly** — a class, a workshop,
  a promo, a menu, a service — with no link in bio and "DM me" as the only route.
- LinkedIn: search posts from your city containing "registration ongoing",
  "slots left", "DM to register", "training in [city]".

**What you record:** the offer, how many times they posted it, where the route
ends.

## Tier 2.1 — Google cannot explain them

**Where:** Google Maps, twenty minutes, very productive.

- Google Maps: search `[trade] near [area]` — salons, clinics, schools, tutors,
  repair services, restaurants, event vendors, photographers.
- Open each listing and look for: **no website link**, fewer than 10 reviews, a
  business name with no service words in it ("Bimpe Enterprises" instead of
  "Bimpe Hair Studio — Braids in Ado-Ekiti"), no opening hours, no photos of the
  work, no description.
- The listing usually carries a **phone number** — that is your WhatsApp/call
  channel, and it is public business information they put there to be contacted.
- Then Google their business name in quotes: `"Bimpe Enterprises" Ado-Ekiti`. If
  page one is a competitor or nothing at all, they are Tier 2.1 with a Tier 1.2
  problem hiding behind it.

**What you record:** business name, area, phone from the listing, review count,
whether the name search returns them first.

## Tier 2.2 — they sound like everybody

**Where:** their About page, LinkedIn company pages, Instagram bios.

- Phrases that flag this tier: "quality service at an affordable price", "we offer
  a wide range of", "best in town", "customer satisfaction is our priority",
  "one-stop shop".
- LinkedIn: search people in your city with titles like Founder, CEO, Owner whose
  About section is a list of services with no customer in it.

**What you record:** the exact phrase, your rewrite in their customer's words.

## Tier 2.3 — daily posts, flat captions

**Where:** Instagram, Facebook, TikTok.

- Businesses that post 4+ times a week (they have the discipline, not the words).
- Captions that are only emojis and a price, or one sentence with no next step, or
  the same caption copied on every post.

**What you record:** their last caption, your rewrite (hook + one useful line +
one next step).

## Tier 3 — do not chase

Established brands with in-house marketers, agencies, and businesses with steady
referrals and clear pages. If they come to you, that is custom work with a scoped
quote — never a ₦5,000 pack in the first message.

---

## The daily routine (20 minutes)

| Minutes | Do |
|---|---|
| 0-5 | Google Maps: 5 listings for one trade in one area → record name, area, phone, review count |
| 5-12 | Instagram: 5 pages from one trade hashtag → record handle + the line you will rewrite |
| 12-17 | Write the 5-10 rewrites. This is the actual work; do not skip it |
| 17-20 | Send the Tier 1 messages first, then Tier 2. Log each send in `LEAD_SHEET.csv` |

Send no more than **15 first touches a day** on WhatsApp from one number. More
than that reads as spam, gets you blocked, and burns the number.

## Rules

- **Public business information only.** Numbers a business published on its own
  listing or page. Never bought lists, never scraped private profiles.
- **One message per business per day**, and every first message contains something
  written for *them*. A generic blast is the villain we are fighting.
- **No results claims.** You may say what the files are and what they fix. You may
  not say "this doubled somebody's sales" — we have no such client and we do not
  invent one.
- **Stop on "not now."** Three touches (1-3-7), then rest the lead for 90 days.
