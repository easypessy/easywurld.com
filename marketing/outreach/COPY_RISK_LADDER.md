# Copywriting outreach: the risk ladder

Who needs copy most, and who does not. Work the list top-down: the people at the
top are losing money **today** on words they already have; the people at the
bottom are fine and should not be chased with a ₦5,000 pack.

Nothing here invents a client, a result or a number. Every tier is scored on what
can be **seen** on the business in two minutes — their page, their bio, their
replies, their Google listing.

Scoring is five yes/no questions. One point each. **5 = bleeding, 0 = fine.**

| # | Question | Where you look |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Do enquiries arrive but die in chat? ("DM for price", slow or inconsistent replies, no follow-up) | their WhatsApp/IG replies, their own words |
| 2 | Does their homepage or bio fail the ten-second test? (what they sell, who it is for, what to do next) | their site, their IG/LinkedIn bio |
| 3 | Is there an offer with nowhere to send people? (a promo, a class, a service — but no page, no price, no next step) | their last 9 posts |
| 4 | Can a stranger find them on Google and understand them there? (no listing, no service words, few reviews) | Google search of their business name |
| 5 | Do they sound like everybody else? ("quality services", "best in town", "we offer a wide range") | their captions and About page |

## TIER 1 — bleeding now (contact this week)

### 1.1 Chat sellers with "DM for price" — score 4-5
Fashion, food, beauty, wigs and lashes, phone accessories, resellers, home
services. Enquiries arrive all day, replies are slow, quotes get sent and never
followed up.

- **What it costs them:** every enquiry is attention they already paid for. A
  buyer who sees "DM for price" passes even with money in hand — that is their
  own customers saying it, not us.
- **What we send:** `PLAYBOOK` — The Chat-to-Customer Playbook (₦5,000). Reply
  flow, 36 scripts, follow-up tracker, WhatsApp Business setup.
- **Why first:** the fix is usable on their very next chat. Fastest yes, fastest
  proof, no website needed.
- **First move:** rewrite their price reply for them, free, in the message.

### 1.2 Websites that talk about the business, not the visitor — score 3-5
Vague headline, no clear next step, credentials before the offer.

- **What it costs them:** visitors leave confused in ten seconds and call the
  competitor whose page made sense.
- **What we send:** `CLEAR` — The Clear Homepage Blueprint (₦5,000). Structure,
  fill-in copy, a scored audit they run themselves.
- **First move:** rewrite their headline in the message, before/after, two lines.

### 1.3 One offer, nowhere to send people — score 3-4
A workshop, a class, a service, a promo — posted repeatedly, with "DM me" as the
only route.

- **What it costs them:** every interested person has to start a conversation;
  the shy ones and the busy ones never do.
- **What we send:** `PAGE` — The One-Offer Landing Page Kit (₦5,000).
- **First move:** write the one sentence their page would open with.

## TIER 2 — losing quietly (contact this month)

### 2.1 Local businesses Google cannot explain — score 2-4
Salons, clinics, schools, tutors, repair services, restaurants, event vendors in
Lagos, Ado-Ekiti, Ibadan, Abuja. Listing missing, thin, or full of words nobody
searches.

- **What we send:** `LOCAL` — The Lagos Local Visibility Starter (₦5,000), then
  `CLEAR` for the page behind it.
- **Note:** the copy problem here is invisible to them. Lead with the search
  test: "Google your business name right now."

### 2.2 Founders who sound like everybody — score 2-3
They change their message every month, or every post says "quality service at an
affordable price".

- **What we send:** `MESSAGE` — The Clear Message Workbook (₦5,000).
- **Note:** slower yes. They must admit the problem before the pack makes sense.
  Sell the customer-interview kit inside it, not "positioning".

### 2.3 Consistent posters with generic captions — score 2-3
They show up daily, hooks are flat, captions explain nothing.

- **What we send:** `ENGINE` — The 30-Day Content Engine (₦5,000), with the
  Hooks and Caption Swipe File as the reason.

## TIER 3 — copy is an upgrade, not a rescue (only if they come to us)

### 3.1 Steady referral businesses — score 1-2
Word of mouth feeds them. Copy raises conversion, not survival. Honest framing:
"This will not change your month. It will make the enquiries you already get
convert better."

### 3.2 Brand-new businesses with no audience — score 1-2
They need to be seen before they need to sound good. Sell `LAUNCH` or `LOCAL`
first; do not sell a homepage rewrite to somebody with no traffic.

### 3.3 Established brands with in-house marketers — score 0-1
**Do not chase with a ₦5,000 pack.** This is custom work: a scoped project with a
custom demo for their business, quoted separately. Sending a DIY pack here costs
us credibility.

## Rules for the whole ladder

1. **Demonstrate before you pitch.** Every Tier 1 and Tier 2 first message
   contains a rewrite of *their* words — their bio, headline, price reply or
   caption. That is the custom demo for their business, and it costs us nothing.
2. **Name the villain, never the person.** "Your price reply loses the sale" —
   not "you are bad at this". The problem is the missing words, not the owner.
3. **Tangible outcomes only.** What they get: replies that answer, a page that
   explains in ten seconds, a next step people can take, a listing that shows up.
   No promised sales, no invented results, no "10x".
4. **₦5,000 is a boundary.** The packs are DIY resources. Custom work is quoted
   separately, and we say so in the same message.
5. **Stop when they say stop.** One follow-up sequence (1-3-7), then the lead is
   closed as "not now" and revisited in 90 days. No fourth message.
6. **Log every contact** in `LEAD_SHEET.csv` the same day, with the tier, the
   channel and the next follow-up date.
