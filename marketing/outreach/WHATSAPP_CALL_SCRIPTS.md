# WhatsApp and call scripts — copywriting first, by tier

Send the message, not the pitch. Every Tier 1 and Tier 2 first touch carries a
**free rewrite of their own words** — that is the custom demo for their business,
and it is the whole reason these get answered.

Fill the square brackets. Keep it under 90 words on WhatsApp. Never promise sales,
never invent a client, never call the pack custom work.

## Calls

### Opener (20 seconds, then stop talking)
> Good afternoon, is that [Name] of [business]? It is [Abu] from Easywurld. I will
> be quick and I am not calling to beg you to buy anything. I looked at your
> [page / website / listing] this morning and I wrote one line that would work
> better for you. Can I read it to you, thirty seconds?

**Then read the rewrite. Then silence.** Their answer tells you the tier.

- If they ask "how much is it?" → the pack answer:
  > The line is free. The pack that produces thirty of them is ₦5,000, files you
  > use yourself, delivered today. Custom work on your business is separate and I
  > would quote it properly. Which one do you want?
- If they say "send it to my WhatsApp" → send the Tier message for their tier
  within five minutes, while the call is warm.
- If they say "we have somebody doing this" → close clean:
  > Good, keep them. Use the line anyway — if it works, you know where I am.

### Voicemail (never longer than this)
> [Name], it is [Abu] from Easywurld. I am not calling to sell. I rewrote one line
> of your [page / listing] and I want to send it to you free — no charge, no
> follow-up if you do not want it. I will send it to this number on WhatsApp now.
> Thank you.

**Then send the WhatsApp message immediately.** The call is only a reason to look
at the message.

### Gatekeeper / "who is calling?"
> Easywurld — we write the words for small businesses. I have something written for
> [business name] that I want to hand over free. It takes one minute. Is [owner]
> around, or should I send it to WhatsApp?

## Objections, answered in their language

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "I will get back to you" | "Sure. One thing before you go: is your price reply answering the question, or is it sending them to DM? If it is answering, you do not need me this month." (then label **Not now**, 1-3-7) |
| "Market is dry / no money" | "I hear you, and I am not going to argue with it. That is exactly why the ₦5,000 is files you use today, not a project you wait for. If even that is not the month, tell me and I will close it clean." |
| "Is it worth it?" | "I cannot promise you sales and I will not. What it does: your enquiries get a clear answer fast, and your page says what you sell in ten seconds. Those are the two places the money leaks. You decide if that is worth ₦5,000." |
| "Can you just do it for me?" | "Yes, and that is custom work, so it is outside the ₦5,000. I will send a short scoped quote for your business only — what gets made, what is not included, when. Give me a day." |
| "How do I know you are not a scam?" | "Fair question. Nothing here is hosted by us and nothing is auto-sent: you get files, you keep your accounts, and you can pay after you see the contents list. Start with the free rewrite — if it is useless, you have lost nothing." |
| "I don't have a website" | "Then do not buy the website pack. Start with the chat replies or the Google listing — those are where your customers already are." |
| "Is this AI?" | "A drafting tool writes the first draft and a person checks every line before you see it. That check is the reason it is usable. We sell the finished words, not the tool." |

## Follow-up sequence (1-3-7, then stop)

**Day 1** (same evening, if they replied at all):
> One more thing I noticed on your [page]: [specific, small, fixable]. Fix that one
> tonight, it costs nothing.

**Day 3** (value, no ask):
> [Name], no selling in this message. Here is the price reply I use for businesses
> like yours — copy it and edit it into your voice: "[script]". If it works, tell
> me. If you want the other 35, that is the PLAYBOOK, ₦5,000.

**Day 7** (close with an easy exit):
> Last message from me on this, I promise. If this month is not the month, reply
> "not now" and I will leave you alone until you message me. If it is, reply
> PLAYBOOK and I will send the account details.

**After day 7:** label `Not now`, revisit in 90 days. No fourth message.
