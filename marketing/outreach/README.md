# Outreach kit — copywriting first, most-needing to least

Four files, one order of work. Built on the risk ladder: contact the people whose
words are costing them money **today** first, and do not chase the people who are
already fine with a ₦5,000 pack.

| File | What it is | When you use it |
|---|---|---|
| `COPY_RISK_LADDER.md` | Who needs copy most → least, with a five-question risk score and the pack that fits each tier | once, to sort every contact you find |
| `FINDING_LEADS.md` | The 20-minute daily routine that fills the sheet with real, reachable contacts, tier by tier | every morning |
| `LEAD_SHEET.csv` | The log: contact, tier, score, the line you rewrote, sends, follow-ups, status, paid, delivered | continuously — nothing is worked that is not in here |
| `WHATSAPP_CALL_SCRIPTS.md` | First touches per tier, call opener, voicemail, gatekeeper, objections, 1-3-7 follow-ups, delivery message | every send |
| `AUTO_RESPONSE.md` | What fires when they reply: Layer 0 instant auto-answer, Layer 1 human within 3 hours, Layer 2 custom demo within a day — plus the WhatsApp Business texts to paste in today | set up once, then it runs |

## The loop in one line

Find (20 min) → score with the ladder → write **their** rewrite → send the tier
message → Layer 0 answers instantly → you answer properly within 3 hours → 1-3-7
follow-up → deliver → log the four Friday numbers in `../MEASUREMENT.csv`.

## Two rules that hold the whole kit together

1. **The demo comes before the pitch.** Nobody gets a price without first getting
   a line of copy written for their business. That is the custom demo, and it is
   free.
2. **Automatic acknowledges, human sells.** Auto-replies answer "how much" and ask
   one qualifying question. Price changes, custom scope, promises and complaints
   are never automated — that boundary is the product.

Honest limits, stated once: no invented clients, no invented results, no promised
sales, ₦5,000 packs are DIY files and not custom work, and no real phone numbers
are supplied here — `FINDING_LEADS.md` is how you collect them from public business
listings and pages in 20 minutes a day.
