## LinkedIn

Here is a test you can run tonight, with no tools and no budget.

Open your homepage. Hand the phone to someone who doesn't know your business. Say nothing. Count to ten.

If they ask "so what exactly do you do?", the headline is the problem — not the colours, not the layout, not the fonts.

Most owners fail this test for one reason: the first screen talks about the company instead of the customer. "We are a creative studio passionate about excellence" tells me nothing about whether you can help me this month.

Rewrite it the way a customer would describe you to a friend:

"We build websites for Lagos businesses that need customers, not compliments."

Same company. Clearer promise. Ten seconds. No guessing.

Then make the decision easy: one button, one destination. A WhatsApp link beats a form nobody finishes. Five options is the same as no options.

Run the test before you spend money on a redesign. Half of the redesigns I'm asked for are headline problems.

## Slide copy (for reference / edits)

1. **Open your homepage. Can a stranger tell what you sell in ten seconds?**
2. **And then they blame the design.** — It is rarely the colours. It is that the first screen is about you, not about the person deciding whether to trust you.
3. **Hand your phone to someone who doesn't know your business.** — Say nothing. Don't explain. Count to ten in your head and watch their eyes, not their mouth.
4. **“So… what do you do exactly?”** — That sentence is the whole diagnosis. Your headline is the problem, not your layout. The layout is doing what it was told.
5. **Say it the way a customer would say it.** — Not “We craft digital experiences.” Try “We build websites for Lagos businesses that need customers, not compliments.” Same company. Clearer promise.
6. **One button, one destination.** — “Message us on WhatsApp” beats a form nobody finishes. If they have to choose between five options, they choose none.
7. **Run the test. Ten seconds. Be honest.** — If it fails, rewrite the headline before you touch the design. It costs nothing and it is where the customers were hiding. *Message CLEAR on WhatsApp and I'll run the test with you.*
