## LinkedIn

Same business. Same products. Two homepages. Only one of them gets messages.

I rebuilt a small food business's homepage this year without touching their brand. Same colours, same tone, same products. Four decisions did the work:

1. The first line stopped describing and started promising. "Quality you can trust" became "Cakes for 20–200 people, delivered across Lagos by 9am."
2. The first photo became the best photo — the cake people actually order, shot in daylight, cut open.
3. The price stopped hiding. "From ₦45,000 for 20 guests" filters out the wrong customers and reassures the right ones.
4. Four menus became one button: "Order on WhatsApp", pre-filled so the customer only has to press send.

Nothing here required a bigger budget. It required deciding what the page is for.

If your page feels "fine" but quiet, start with the first line. It is the cheapest change with the loudest effect.

## Slide copy (for reference / edits)

1. **Same business. Same products. Two homepages.** — One gets messages. One gets silence. The difference is four decisions, not a budget.
2. **The first line stopped describing and started promising.** — Before: “Quality you can trust.” After: “Cakes for 20–200 people, delivered across Lagos by 9am.” One is a mood. The other is an offer.
3. **The first photo became the best photo.** — Instead of a stock kitchen, the cake people actually order — shot on a phone, in daylight, cut open so you can see the layers.
4. **The price stopped hiding.** — “From ₦45,000 for 20 guests” filters out the people who would waste your day, and reassures the ones who can afford you.
5. **One button replaced four menus.** — “Order on WhatsApp” — and it opens with the message already half written, so the customer only has to send it.
6. **The brand stayed the same.** — Same colours, same tone, same business. We removed hesitation, not personality. That is the whole trick.
7. **Pick one of the four and do it today.** — Start with the first line. It is the cheapest change with the loudest effect. *Message CLEAR on WhatsApp if you want a before/after review.*
