# The daily system — 11 carousels a day

This is the structure. Every day gets the same eleven slots, in the same order,
with a different idea in each. The slots never change — that is what makes the
system producible. The ideas rotate, so the audience never sees the same post
twice.

---

## Before the structure: one honest warning

You asked for **5 LinkedIn business posts per day**. LinkedIn will not thank you
for it. Posting more than twice a day from the same page splits your reach,
looks automated, and burns the audience that matters most to you.

So the system keeps your eleven assets a day, but distributes the five business
ones:

- **2 go to LinkedIn first** (the diagnose and the proof).
- **3 go to Instagram, Facebook and TikTok photo-mode** with a native caption.
- Every asset still carries a LinkedIn caption, so you can promote any of them
  later if it performs.

That gives you eleven finished carousels a day, five of them business-focused,
without getting muted.

The other warning, plainly: **eleven designs a day is 330 a month.** Nobody
sustains that alone. Start with **one full day a week** (11 posts, spread across
seven days), build the habit, then increase. The structure below works at either
cadence — the slots do not care how often you run them.

## Why website design and copywriting are the right doors

Your instinct is right, with one correction.

**They are doors because they are diagnosable.** A website and its words are the
only two things a stranger judges before you ever speak. That makes them the
easiest place to *point at a problem* — and pointing at a problem is what earns
the right to sell the next thing.

**But two doors at once makes the shop look crowded.** Your own site already
lists eight services equally; that is the "crowded" feeling you described. Pick
one door per channel:

- **LinkedIn → website design.** Decision-makers with budget. Higher ticket,
  produces screenshot-proof content, and reveals every other gap.
- **WhatsApp / Instagram → copywriting.** Fast, cheap to try, immediate
  before/after ("send me your homepage headline, I'll rewrite it"). This is how
  you get a client who cannot afford a site yet.

**The ladder, in order.** Never sell the third rung first:

```
Audit (free or ₦15k)  →  Door (site or copy)  →  Retainer (SMM)  →  Systems (AI automation, reports)
        trust                  clarity                 consistency            leverage
```

- **SMM** sells itself after a website, because you are already holding their
  brand and their assets. Selling it cold is the hardest sale you will ever make.
- **AI automation** sells after a retainer, because by then you know their
  bottleneck by name — the enquiry that never gets a reply, the report built by
  hand every Friday.
- **Reports** sell to clients who already pay you and want to look good upward.

**The handoff is one page.** At the end of every project, give the client a
single sheet: *"Three more things we noticed"* — with the cost of each. No
pitch, no meeting. That sheet is your upsell, and it converts because it is
evidence, not enthusiasm.

## How a day is built (the production loop)

```
marketing/november-2026/
├── days/
│   └── day-02/
│       ├── 01-linkedin-diagnose/     slides + CAPTION.md
│       ├── 02-linkedin-teach/
│       ├── 03-personal-story/
│       ├── 04-linkedin-proof/
│       ├── 05-status-lesson/
│       ├── 06-opinion/
│       ├── 07-personal-belief/
│       ├── 08-status-proof/
│       ├── 09-linkedin-invitation/
│       ├── 10-personal-behind/
│       └── 11-easywurld/
├── days.mjs          ← every word of every day
└── build/render.mjs  ← renders all days
```

1. **Pick the eleven angles** from the bank above (one per slot).
2. **Write them into `days.mjs`** — headline, body, one line of foot.
3. **Pick the screenshots** for slots 4 and 8 from `client/public/shots/`.
4. **Render**: `node marketing/november-2026/build/render.mjs day-02`.
5. **Read each `CAPTION.md`**, replace the platform captions if you want a
   different voice, then schedule.

through `day-11…`), which shows every slot filled with a finished example.

## What makes the eleven feel like one studio

- Same layout, same footer, same type, same one terracotta accent.
- Business slots 1, 2, 4, 6 argue. Personal slots 3, 7, 10 confess. Status slots
  5, 8 repeat the argument in twenty seconds. Slot 11 introduces the person who
  is doing all of it.
- No slot sells the retainer. The retainer is sold by the *pattern* — eleven
  pieces of evidence that you think clearly.
