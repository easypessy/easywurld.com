# The daily timetable

15 touches a day: **11 feed carousels** (TikTok 4 · Instagram 4 · Facebook 3),
**3 stories**, **1 LinkedIn post**. Every slot has a time because of what the
audience is doing at that hour — not because the clock looks tidy.

All times **WAT (Africa/Lagos)**.

## What each weekday argues

Rotating the theme stops the week feeling like one long complaint.

| Day | Theme | What that means for the slots |
|---|---|---|
| **Mon** | Clarity reset | Diagnose is about the homepage; Teach is the ten-second test |
| **Tue** | Price and perception | Diagnose is "DM for price"; Teach is how to show a range |
| **Wed** | **Proof day** | Slot 4 leads; slot 11 runs as the Easywurld post; Teach is a teardown |
| **Thu** | Story day | Personal story goes earlier (12:00 IG) because it is the strongest asset |

## The Easywurld post (the one that gets clients)

A page that only gives advice gets followed. A page that talks about its own
marketing business gets **hired** — that is what you noticed, and it is true.

**Every Wednesday, slot 11 is about Easywurld itself**, rotating:

| Week | Angle | What it answers |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The story + location** — how it started, why the name, where it works | "Who are these people?" |
| 2 | What "digital studio" means | "What do you actually do?" |
| 3 | The process: understand, simplify, build, optimize | "How would you work with me?" |
| 4 | The price — ranges, and what moves them | "Can I afford this?" |
| 5 | The handover — what the client owns | "What do I get?" |
| 6 | The refusal — work we turn down | "Are they serious?" |
| 7 | The first 14 days after payment | "What happens if I say yes?" |

Built so far:
- `day-11-easywurld-introduction` — what we are, in one argument
- `day-12-easywurld-story-and-location` — the story, the name, the place

## The three stories (they do more selling than the posts)

| Time | Story | Job |
|---|---|---|
| 07:30 | One line from today's post + a poll ("Does your site show a price? Yes / No / Scared to") | Wake the audience, collect data |
| 12:30 | A screenshot from the proof post + "message CLEAR" sticker | Turn a scroll into a DM |
| 18:00 | Behind the scenes: the desk, a draft, a mistake, a decision | Make the studio feel like people |

Stories are where you can be **less polished and more frequent**. Three a day is
the minimum for a business account; they cost almost nothing to make.

## Mechanics that matter more than the schedule

1. **First 30 minutes.** Reply to every comment. The algorithm reads conversation,
   not likes. If you cannot reply, do not post.
2. **One idea per asset.** If the carousel needs a second idea, it is two posts.
3. **The save is the goal.** Every caption ends with "save this" or a reason to
   send it to one specific person.
4. **Rewrite the first line per platform.** Same body, new hook.
5. **Batch on Sunday.** Eleven carousels take about two hours once the words
   exist; scheduling takes twenty minutes.
6. **Track four numbers per slot after two weeks** — saves, shares, DMs, follows.
   Cut the two weakest slots and double the strongest. The grid is a starting
   hypothesis, not scripture.
7. **Never post the same image twice in a week** unless it earned it.

---

## Honest note about volume

15 touches a day is a full content operation. If you are doing this alone, run
the **full grid on Wednesday (proof day) and Saturday (Lagos day)** and a
reduced grid — 5 posts, 3 stories, 1 LinkedIn — on the other days. Same system,
sustainable rhythm. The slots do not expire.
