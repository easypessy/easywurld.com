# “This is too simple.” She didn't pay me

**Monday 16 November · Personal (LinkedIn + Instagram) · 1080×1350 carousel · 7 slides**

> A story about being wrong. Do not soften the ending — the lesson only works if you admit the mistake first.

---

## LinkedIn

A client once told me my work was "too simple". She didn't pay.

Month three of my business. I had built her five sections, a moving header, a scroll animation I was genuinely proud of. Two weeks of work.

Her customers kept asking where the prices were.

I argued. Then I opened it on my own phone, outside, on mobile data. Half the page never loaded. The menu disappeared. The only thing that worked was the animation.

She was right. I was designing for the compliment, not for the customer.

The lesson took a while to accept: simple is not lazy. Simple is what survives — a slow network, a cracked screen, a distracted person scrolling in traffic. Decoration survives none of those.

Now I build every page on the worst phone I can find before I call it finished. If it reads clearly there, it works everywhere.

If you're a designer reading this: the client who rejects your cleverest work is often doing you a favour.

If you're a business owner: the page that feels "too simple" is usually the one that sells.

## Slide copy (for reference / edits)

1. **“This is too simple.” She didn't pay me.**
2. **I had built her five sections and a moving header.** — I was proud of it. I had spent two weeks on details nobody would ever notice, and I was ready to be told so.
3. **Customers kept asking where the prices were.** — Not one person had mentioned the animation. They wanted to know if they could afford her — and how fast she could deliver.
4. **Then I opened it on my own phone, outside, on data.** — Half the page never loaded. The menu disappeared. The only thing that worked properly was the thing I was proudest of.
5. **Simple is not lazy. Simple is what survives.** — It survives a slow network, a cracked screen, a distracted customer and a busy Monday. Decoration survives none of those.
6. **I build the page on the worst phone I can find.** — If it still reads clearly there, the design is finished. If it only works on my laptop, it is decoration.
7. **Send this to someone who confuses decoration with design.** — Or open your own page on data, outside, right now. You'll see it in ten seconds. *Message CLEAR on WhatsApp if you want a second opinion.*
