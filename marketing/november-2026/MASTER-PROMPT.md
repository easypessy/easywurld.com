## 2. Visual rules (never break)

| Token | Hex | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Cream | `#FAE7C7` | every background |
| Deep green | `#0A4937` | headlines, footer |
| Leaf green | `#27473A` | mark, buttons |
| Terracotta | `#9E4E2C` | **one** accent per slide |
| Ink | `#14170F` | body copy |

- Type: **Fraunces** (headlines, wordmark), **Inter** (body), **JetBrains Mono**
  (labels, numbers, footer). Never substitute.
- Logo: real mark only (`client/public/brand/mark.png`). Never redraw, recolour,
  stretch or put on a different background.
- Formats: **1080×1350** feed carousels, **1080×1920** status/story.
- The footer is fixed: `EASYWURLD · SIMPLIFY. OPTIMIZE. GROW. · 03 / 07`.
- Slide 1 is mostly empty. That emptiness is the design.

## 3. Voice rules

- Say the number: ₦45,000, twenty guests, ten seconds, 9am.
- One sentence per line. One idea per slide.
- Use the customer's own words as quotes ("DM for price", "too simple").
- Admit cost. "I argued. Then I opened it on my phone." Beats being right.
- Nigerian business English. Pidgin only if the hook needs it, never as costume.
- **Banned words:** unlock, elevate, seamless, cutting-edge, game-changer,
  revolutionary, "in today's fast-paced world", "let's dive in".
- **Never invent:** client names, testimonials, revenue, rankings, percentages.
  Concept work is always labelled **concept build · sample build**.

## 4. The funnel (positioning)

Two doors, one ladder, never sell the third rung first.

```
Audit (free or ₦15k) → Door (website or copywriting) → Retainer (SMM) → Systems (AI automation, reports)
```

- **LinkedIn door = website design.** Higher ticket, decision-makers, produces
  screenshot-proof content, exposes every other gap.
- **WhatsApp / Instagram door = copywriting.** Cheap to try, instant
  before/after ("send me your headline, I'll rewrite one line"). Catches clients
  who cannot afford a site yet.
- **SMM** is sold after a website, when we already hold their brand assets.
- **AI automation** is sold after a retainer, when we can name the bottleneck.
- The handoff is **one page**: "Three more things we noticed", with the cost of
  each. No meeting, no pitch.

## 5. The eleven slots a day

Every day: **5 business · 3 personal · 2 status · 1 Easywurld.**

| # | Slot | Platform | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Diagnose | LinkedIn | They recognise themselves in a mistake |
| 2 | Teach | LinkedIn | One method they can use today (copywriting door) |
| 3 | Personal — story | IG + LinkedIn | A real moment, a mistake, what it cost |
| 4 | Proof | LinkedIn | Real work, screenshotted and annotated |
| 5 | Status — lesson | WhatsApp + Stories | One idea in 20 seconds, ends "message CLEAR" |
| 6 | Opinion | IG + LinkedIn | The contrarian take that sets our standard |
| 7 | Personal — belief | IG | What we refuse to do |
| 8 | Status — proof | WhatsApp + Stories | A screenshot, one line, one ask |
| 9 | Invitation | LinkedIn | Useful reply-bait — the soft sell |
| 10 | Personal — behind the scenes | IG + TikTok | Work in progress, a boring truth |
| 11 | Easywurld | LinkedIn, pinned | Who we are, how we work, price, handover |

LinkedIn gets **two** of the five business posts a day, not five: more than that
splits reach and looks automated. The other three go to Instagram, Facebook and
TikTok photo-mode with native captions.

## 6. Hook formulas

| Formula | Example |
|---|---|
| Callout | "Your website is why they picked your competitor." |
| Audit | "I opened 20 Lagos business sites. 17 had the same problem." |
| Cost | "Your website is losing you customers every day. Here's the leak." |
| 10-second test | "Can a stranger tell what you sell in ten seconds?" |
| Before/after | "Same business. Same products. Two homepages." |
| Myth-break | "You don't need a bigger website. You need a clearer one." |
| Reply bait | "Drop your link. I'll tell you the first thing I'd fix." |
| Confession | "I almost quit in month three." |

Test rule: **same body, three hooks**, three weeks apart. Track **saves and
shares**, not likes.

## 7. Carousel structure

| Slide | Job | Never |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | One feeling, under twelve words | Explain, hedge, introduce yourself |
| 2 | The pain in the customer's words | Be polite |
| 3–6 | One idea + one boring, specific example | Two ideas, abstract advice |
| 7 | Ask for the send, then one small step | Ask for a sale, list five links |

Five-slide version for statuses: hook → idea → proof → flip → ask.

Story spine for personal posts: **ordinary scene → the mistake → the evidence →
what it cost → the rule you kept.**

## 8. Caption structure

| Platform | Length | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | 120–220 words | Line 1 = the hook again. Short lines. Specific ask. 3–5 hashtags at the end. |
| Instagram | 60–100 words | Hook, three beats, "save this", CTA, 8 hashtags. |
| TikTok / Reels | 20–40 words | Timestamped beats + on-screen text. |
| WhatsApp | 40–80 words | Like a message to one person. No hashtags. Ends "message CLEAR". |

## 9. Prompt template (use this to write any slide)

```
You are writing slide [N] of a 7-slide Easywurld carousel.

Audience: Lagos small-business owners with a website or about to buy one.
Sceptical, practical, reading on a phone.

Voice: plain, specific, occasionally blunt. Nigerian business English. No jargon.

Post hook: "[HOOK]"
This slide's job: [hook / pain / point / proof / share / CTA]
This slide's one idea: [ONE SENTENCE]

Rules: headline under 22 words, body under 45. One idea. One boring specific
example (a price, a time, a quote). No invented stats, names or results. Never
use: unlock, elevate, seamless, game-changer, dive in. End with the line the
reader would repeat to a friend.

Return: headline, body, one-line art direction.
```

## 10. Images

- Illustration: drawn in code (browser, phone, clock, before/after, seal) — it
  can never contradict the words or misspell the brand.
- Proof: **real screenshots** from `client/public/shots/`, inside a browser
  frame, labelled **concept build**.
- Photography, when used, is our own work — real screens, real products, real
  shopfronts. No stock imagery that implies a client we do not have.
