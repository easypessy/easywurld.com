## LinkedIn

Three questions run through a stranger's head in the first ten seconds on your homepage. They never say them out loud:

1. What exactly do you sell?
2. How much is it, and what do I get?
3. What do I do next?

Most small-business sites answer none of them above the fold. The owner thinks the site is "fine" because it looks clean. Clean is not the same as clear.

Open yours on a phone, on data, and read only the first screen. If a stranger would have to scroll or guess to answer those three, that is the work.

## Slide copy (for reference / edits)

1. **Your homepage has ten seconds. Maybe less.**
2. **They are asking one question.** — Not “is this nice?” — they already decided that. They are asking whether you are for them.
3. **“What exactly do you sell?”** — If the answer is a slogan, they leave. Say the thing plainly, in the words a customer uses.
4. **“How much, and what do I get?”** — You don't have to publish every price. You do have to stop making people guess.
5. **“What do I do next?”** — One button. Message CLEAR on WhatsApp and I'll tell you which of the three your page fails.
