## How to post a day

1. Open `days/day-07/` (or whichever day).
2. Post `01-*.png` first, then the rest in order.
3. Copy the caption for the platform you are posting to.
4. Post the eleven slots at the times in `TIMETABLE.md`.

## Before you post

- Sector figures (₦45,000, ₦2.5m, and so on) are **illustrative examples**.
  Replace them with real market numbers, or delete the figure.
- Every screenshot is labelled **concept build** on the slide. Do not remove
  the label unless the work is a real, approved client build.
