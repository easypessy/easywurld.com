## LinkedIn

A parent asked a Lagos school for fees last week. The website did not answer.

No fee range. No "what's included". No next step. So they asked another school — not a better one, just a clearer one.

This is the most common leak I find in education, healthcare and professional services: the price is treated as a secret to be protected, when it is actually the first trust signal.

Three things fix it, and none requires publishing sensitive numbers:

1. A range, plus what it covers. "From ₦150,000 per term, includes books and lunch."
2. A list of what is extra. Transport, exam fees, uniforms. One line each.
3. The next step, in one sentence. "Book a tour on WhatsApp; we reply within a day."

A parent will forgive a price they can see. They do not forgive a price they have to dig for, in a conversation they were not ready to have.

If you run a school, clinic or consultancy in Lagos: open your site on your phone tonight and ask whether a stranger could answer "how much, and what do I get?" without calling you.

## Slide copy (for reference / edits)

1. **A parent asked your school for fees. Your website didn't answer.**
2. **So they asked another school.** — Not because yours is worse. Because the other one had a fee summary and a phone number they could see at 11pm, without calling anybody.
3. **“Fees change, so we don't publish them.”** — Then publish the range and what it covers: per term, what is included, what is extra. Parents are not scared of a number. They are scared of a conversation they cannot prepare for.
4. **“What do I get for it?”** — Uniform, books, transport, meals, exam fees — one line each. A parent will forgive ₦150,000 if they can see what it buys. They will not forgive guessing.
5. **“What happens after I call?”** — A tour, an assessment, a form? Say it in one sentence. Silence is where the enquiry dies.
6. **Put the range, the inclusions and the next step on one page.** — Not the whole fee structure. One page. That is the difference between a parent who calls and a parent who forgets you. *Message CLEAR on WhatsApp if you want a second pair of eyes.*
