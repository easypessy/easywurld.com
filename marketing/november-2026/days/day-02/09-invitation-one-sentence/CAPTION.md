2. **If it needs a paragraph, it isn't clear yet.** — The sentence is not for your customers. It is for you — it is the thing you should be able to say at a party, in traffic, on the phone.
3. **Who it's for, what it does, what changes.** — “Cakes for Lagos offices that need 50 slices by 10am” passes. “Quality baking services” fails.
4. **One line back, in the comments.** — If it's good, I'll say so. If it isn't, I'll rewrite it — publicly, so everyone else learns too.
