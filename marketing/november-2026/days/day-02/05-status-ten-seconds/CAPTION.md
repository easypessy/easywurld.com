## LinkedIn

Three questions decide whether a stranger stays on your page, and they all happen in about ten seconds.

What do you sell? How much, and what do I get? What happens if I message?

Most business pages answer none of them above the fold, then wonder why the traffic does not convert. The owner reads their own page with all their knowledge and thinks it is obvious. A stranger reads it with none and thinks it is vague.

Test it tonight: hand your phone to someone who does not know the business, say nothing, and count to ten.

## Slide copy (for reference / edits)

1. **A stranger gives your page ten seconds.**
2. **What do you sell?** — In their words, not yours. If it takes a scroll to find out, they are already gone.
3. **How much, and what do I get?** — A range is enough. Silence is not.
4. **What happens if I message?** — One button. One answer. Not a form with eleven fields.
5. **Count to ten on your own page.** — Message CLEAR with the one you failed and I'll tell you how to fix it.
