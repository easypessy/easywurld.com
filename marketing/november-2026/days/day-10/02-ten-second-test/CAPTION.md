## LinkedIn

The ten-second test, in three questions.

What do you sell? What does it cost? What happens if I message? Hand your phone to someone who does not know the business. Say nothing. Count to ten.

Which one they cannot answer. That is your next edit, and it is usually the price.

Run it tonight with one person. Ten seconds of honesty is worth a month of redesign ideas.
