A booking flow with one step fewer.

Create an account before you can see the times. Nobody wants an account. They want to know if Thursday at 4 is free.

See times, pick one, confirm on WhatsApp. Three taps, no password, and the front desk stopped taking bookings by phone.

Count the steps between wanting and booking. Delete one. Then delete another.
