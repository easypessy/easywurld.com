## LinkedIn

How to publish a price range without scaring people.

“₦18,000. Most projects land in the middle because of X.” The range tells people where they are. The reason tells them whether it is fair.

“Below this, we would be guessing — and we would rather say no.” A limit reads as confidence, not arrogance, when it comes with a reason.

Publish the range with one sentence of reasoning. Silence is the only wrong answer.
