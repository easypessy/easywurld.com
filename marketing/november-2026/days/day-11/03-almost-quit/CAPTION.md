## LinkedIn

There was a month I nearly stopped doing this.

Two clients went quiet, one invoice went unpaid, and every project felt like an argument. I told myself the market was bad. Some of it was. Most of it was that I was selling pages instead of outcomes.

I started asking one question before quoting. “What happens in your business if this works?” The answer became the proposal, and the arguments stopped.

Ask that question on your next call. It changes what you write, and what you charge.

If this is your business — a clinic where appointments can only be booked by phone — message CLEAR on WhatsApp and I will send back the first thing I would change. No pitch.
