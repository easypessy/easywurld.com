## LinkedIn

Rewrite your headline in three passes.

Write what you do, plainly. “We bake cakes in Lekki.” Ugly, but true and specific.

Add who it is for and what changes. “Celebration cakes for Lagos offices that need 50 slices by 10am.” Then pass three: cut every word that does not change the meaning.

Do it once. Not five times. Good copy is a decision, not a mood.
