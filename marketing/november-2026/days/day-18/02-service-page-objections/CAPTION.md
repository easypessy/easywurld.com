## LinkedIn

A service page should answer objections before they are spoken.

How long? What if I don't like it? What if it breaks? Can I pay in two parts? Write the answer to each in one line, under the offer.

An answered objection is a closed tab you never see. And it saves you from repeating the same three messages every week.

Write the four questions you are asked most. Put them on the page, in that order.
