## LinkedIn

The 'so what' ladder: five rungs to a real headline.

“We are a design studio.” So what? “We design websites.” So what? “Your site will be clear enough to sell without you explaining it.” Stop when the next answer is a reason to pay.

Write the sentence a customer would repeat to a friend. If it is too long to repeat, it is not a headline yet.

Climb five rungs on your own sentence. The fifth one is usually your homepage.
