# 01 — Market intelligence & strategy

**Owner:** Strategy / insights · **Prerequisites:** 00 · **Pass evidence:** Evidence-backed strategy brief and source ledger

## What this department must know

Strategy is disciplined choice under uncertainty: select a market, audience, problem, position, offer and route to action that can be served credibly. It is not a list of channels, a collection of trends or an AI-generated persona.

### Core knowledge

- **Business model and offer:** customer, user, buyer, beneficiary, value delivered, price/terms, capacity, constraints, alternatives and economics where the client can share them.
- **Market and category:** category entry points (situations that prompt buying), category conventions, competitors/substitutes, regulation, seasonality and structural change. Competitor statements are evidence only after checking source, date and context.
- **Customer understanding:** jobs and circumstances, language, needs, barriers, desired progress, decision process and accessibility. Distinguish observed behavior, stated preference and analyst interpretation.
- **Positioning:** for [audience/situation], [brand/offer] is [meaningful frame] that [distinctive value], supported by [credible proof]. Define alternatives and trade-offs; avoid claiming uniqueness without checking.
- **Demand and funnel:** distinguish awareness, consideration, action, onboarding, retention and referral as useful diagnostic stages—not a universal linear journey. Identify the actual friction and event at each stage.
- **Research methods:** desk research, interviews, observation, surveys, search/query analysis, customer-service and sales records, web analytics and experiments. Select a method based on the decision, not because it is familiar.
- **Local context:** include language, purchasing norms, device/connectivity realities, geography, payment/delivery, trust and cultural context relevant to the actual market. Do not stereotype Nigerians or treat Lagos as a single homogeneous audience.

## Evidence practice

Maintain a source ledger with source/author, publisher, URL or file, publication date, access date, method/sample if relevant, direct finding, limits, permitted inference and the decision it informs. Label items:

- **A — Direct observation:** project/customer data with known provenance and limitations.
- **B — Credible external evidence:** documented study or primary source fit for the claim.
- **C — Practitioner interpretation:** useful framework or informed analysis; not universal law.
- **D — Working hypothesis:** testable explanation or proposal, not a fact.

Prefer primary documents for policy, prices, product features and official data. Prefer systematic reviews and transparent research for broad empirical claims. Check the date and population before applying findings. A quote or viral post is not market research. Never create fictional interviews, survey results, customer quotes or personas and label them real.

## Strategy memo template

1. Decision and date; client/product and market.
2. Objective and business relevance; what is out of scope.
3. Priority audience and situation; evidence and confidence.
4. Problem/opportunity; alternatives and friction.
5. Position and central promise; reasons to believe and qualifications.
6. Offer and route to action; CTA and destination.
7. Message territory and creative hypotheses (2–3, not declared winners).
8. Channel role, customer journey assumption and dependencies.
9. KPI, baseline, event definition, measurement window and attribution caveat.
10. Risks, unknowns, source ledger and next research action.

## Connections to other departments

- **Psychology:** strategy defines the decision and context; psychology offers cautious explanations, not stereotypes.
- **Copy:** position, proof and qualification become the message hierarchy.
- **Design/film:** strategic meaning sets what the viewer must recognize, feel and do; creative is not decoration.
- **Distribution:** audience and channel roles shape discoverability, accessibility and adaptation.
- **Analytics:** a strategy claim must be falsifiable enough to evaluate; measurement can revise the diagnosis.
- **Governance:** source ledger and claims register travel with every campaign.

## Practice lab

For an example small business (mark every invented detail as hypothetical), define a decision question, gather five traceable sources, conduct or design ethical customer discovery, map three alternatives, write two competing positions, and explain what evidence would change your mind. Do not publish sample findings as real client research.

## Assessment

Submit the strategy memo, source ledger and one-page recommendation. Reviewer checks traceability, fit of evidence, clarity of audience and objective, explicit limitations, business relevance, plausible CTA and measurable learning question. **Pass:** no unsupported factual claim; at least two material assumptions stated; recommendation follows from evidence; a disconfirming observation is named. **Remediate:** replace weak source, narrow the claim or gather needed evidence before downstream work begins.
