# 10 — Capstone, rubric & readiness

**Owner:** Curriculum lead + cross-functional review panel · **Prerequisites:** Modules 00–09 · **Pass evidence:** Traceable campaign system and oral defense

## Capstone assignment

Create a **paper campaign system** for a fictional or approved real offer. This is a planning and assessment exercise only: do not generate or publish video, synthetic human media, ads or customer research as if real. Mark all hypothetical business facts as hypothetical. If using a real client, obtain permission and use authorized data only.

### Required artifacts

1. Intake brief and decision log.
2. Strategy memo and source ledger; distinguish evidence, interpretation and hypothesis.
3. Audience decision map and ethical persuasion checklist.
4. Claims register and message architecture.
5. Two creative concepts with visual direction; note brand/client identity and accessibility.
6. If proposing video: full production package per module 05, without generating it; if not, justify the selected format.
7. If proposing a synthetic human: identity bible, permissions/disclosure plan and continuity QA; otherwise explain why not.
8. Channel rationale and adaptation matrix, publishing/moderation plan.
9. Measurement plan, test hypothesis, metric definitions, data/privacy plan and limitations.
10. Release checklist, risk register, named approvers and correction/rollback plan.
11. A five-minute defense: recommendation, strongest evidence, largest uncertainty, ethical trade-off, what would change your mind.

## Scoring rubric (100 points)

| Dimension | Points | Strong evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Audience, objective and business diagnosis | 12 | Specific, evidence-informed, contextual; objective is not a vanity metric |
| Research and source quality | 12 | Traceable, timely, fit-for-purpose; limitations and disconfirming evidence stated |
| Consumer insight and ethical persuasion | 10 | Hypotheses are cautious; choice, dignity and terms are respected |
| Copy, story, message and claim integrity | 12 | Clear hierarchy, truthful proof, paid-off hook, usable CTA and natural language |
| Visual direction and brand/accessibility | 10 | Purposeful, distinctive, legible, on-brief and accessible |
| Film / production craft (if applicable) | 10 | Independent direction; complete timed scene/shot/sound/edit package |
| Synthetic identity and rights (if applicable) | 8 | Consent/provenance and continuity are explicit; no identity leakage |
| Distribution and community readiness | 8 | Channel fit, native adaptation, sustainable operations, moderation and current checks |
| Measurement and learning design | 8 | Defined metrics, valid comparison, privacy and honest causal limits |
| Governance and cross-team handoffs | 10 | Clear ownership, risk handling, approvals, audit trail and correction path |
| **Total** | **100** | |

**Pass threshold:** at least 80/100 and no critical failure. Critical failures override the score: fabricated evidence; unapproved personal/sensitive data use; unsupported material claim; rights/consent unknown for identifiable person or voice; Easywurld character face reveal; hidden material terms; deceptive synthetic media; inaccessible essential information; or a video plan that merely enlarges carousel slides. A critical failure means revise and reassess, not a numeric deduction alone.

## Readiness levels

- **Observer:** may review training examples; cannot approve.
- **Contributor:** may draft within approved briefs; all outputs require specialist review.
- **Practitioner:** may own bounded work in passed discipline with named approver and governance gates.
- **Reviewer:** may assess work in passed disciplines; does not self-approve a high-risk release.
- **Release approver:** formally designated by agency/client; has documented authority and current policy/rights checks.

Readiness is role-specific. Passing copy does not qualify someone to approve synthetic identity, legal claims or analytics privacy. Refresh assessment after a major policy/tool change, extended inactivity or an incident.

## Calibration and remediation

Two reviewers score independently, cite evidence in the artifact and discuss differences over 10 points. Record final score, critical failures, feedback and corrective action. Remediation should target the failed competency and include a new artifact; do not merely repeat the quiz. Keep examples de-identified and permissioned before reuse as training material.

## Graduation condition

The campaign is considered **training-ready**, not automatically launch-ready, when all rubric dimensions pass, all unresolved questions have owners, each relevant specialist approves, and a real campaign still completes its current client, legal, platform, rights and release checks.
