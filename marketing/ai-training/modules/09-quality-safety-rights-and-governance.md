# 09 — Editorial quality, safety, rights & governance

**Owner:** Editorial standards / release governance · **Prerequisites:** 00–08 · **Pass evidence:** Completed release checklist and risk decision record

## Purpose

Protect audience trust, client interests, contributors and the agency. This module supplies a risk-aware process, not legal advice. Requirements depend on jurisdiction, sector, audience, media, contract and platform. Escalate regulated or high-impact work to qualified counsel/compliance reviewers.

## Core controls

### Accuracy and substantiation

- Verify every factual, comparative, performance, price, health, financial, environmental, safety and endorsement claim against current evidence and exact wording.
- Keep a claims register: claim, implied claim, evidence, scope, source/date, owner, qualifier, expiry/review date, allowed placement and approver.
- Quotes/testimonials must be authentic, permissioned, accurately represented and not edited to alter meaning. Disclose material connections and incentives where required.
- AI-generated image/audio/video is not evidence. Clearly label illustrative, fictional or synthetic content where needed to avoid deception or satisfy current rules.

### Rights and provenance

Record source, creator, asset ID, license/permission, permitted formats, channels, territories, term, paid use, edits, attribution and expiration for every material asset: music, footage, photos, fonts, logos, likeness, voice, source code and AI reference assets. Check tool terms and client contract. “Found online,” “AI-generated” or “royalty-free” does not itself establish unrestricted rights.

### Privacy and security

Minimize personal data; obtain appropriate permissions and lawful basis; restrict access; use secure project storage; define retention/deletion; avoid secrets and sensitive customer data in prompts or public tools. Do not create sensitive inferences or target people using sensitive traits. Maintain a process for requests, incidents and deletion consistent with applicable requirements.

### Fairness and audience protection

Review for stereotype, exclusion, accessibility failure, harmful implications, vulnerable audience targeting, unequal access, manipulative design, cultural misrepresentation and inappropriate humor. Make limitations and material terms visible. Consider impact if the creative is seen outside intended context.

### Synthetic media and identity

Apply module 06's consent, identity, provenance and anonymous character requirements. Check the final rendered sequence frame by frame for accidental face reveal, artifact, real-person resemblance or misleading context. Confirm appropriate disclosure and platform labels at release.

### Platform, localization and publication

Check official current platform policies, format rules, ad/partnership disclosures, music use, AI content rules, local laws, language nuance and destination page. Store URL/source, check date and reviewer. Recheck after edits and before launch.

## Release checklist

- [ ] Approved brief, objective, audience, CTA and destination are current.
- [ ] All claims checked; qualifiers intact; sources in claims register.
- [ ] Client facts, names, spelling, prices, terms and URLs confirmed.
- [ ] Content is not misleading by image, sound, omission, edit or implication.
- [ ] Rights, consent, license, territory, term and paid use confirmed.
- [ ] Privacy/security and tracking approved; data minimized.
- [ ] Synthetic identity and disclosure checks completed; Easywurld face remains anonymous.
- [ ] Accessibility checked: contrast, safe zones, captions, transcript, alt text and reduced motion where relevant.
- [ ] Current platform/policy/spec checks recorded; mobile preview reviewed.
- [ ] Music, voice, image and fonts cleared for destination channels.
- [ ] CTA works; destination matches promise; moderation and contact owner ready.
- [ ] Final file/version/checksum and reviewer approval recorded.
- [ ] Rollback/correction plan and escalation contact are available.

## Incident and correction protocol

If a material false claim, rights/privacy issue, face reveal, harmful misrepresentation or security incident appears: pause scheduled distribution where possible; preserve the relevant version and records; notify the accountable owner and appropriate specialist; assess scope and audience impact; correct or remove without erasing the audit trail; inform affected parties when required; document cause and prevention. Do not conceal, blame the model, or silently replace the artifact.

## Connections

Governance is cross-functional, not a final spell-check. Strategy owns factual context, copy owns wording, design/film own representation, identity lead owns synthetic continuity/consent evidence, distribution owns policy and moderation, analytics owns measurement privacy, and release approver owns launch authorization.

## Assessment

Conduct a tabletop review of a hypothetical ad containing an unverified statistic, AI-generated customer portrait, licensed music of unknown term, and tracked link. **Pass:** identifies each issue, blocks release where necessary, assigns owners, specifies evidence or alternative treatment, preserves useful audit details and communicates a clear decision. No “fix” may disguise a material defect.
