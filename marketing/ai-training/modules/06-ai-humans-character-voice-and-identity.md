# 06 — AI-generated humans, character, voice & identity

**Owner:** Synthetic media / identity lead · **Prerequisites:** 00, 04–05, 09 · **Pass evidence:** Approved identity bible, consent/rights record and continuity test

## Scope

This capability covers synthetic or AI-assisted human imagery, character continuity, voice selection and disclosure. Realistic synthetic media can mislead audiences and can implicate likeness, voice, privacy, copyright, publicity, cultural representation and platform rules. Obtain qualified review for live use. The tool being able to generate a person is not permission to do so.

## Easywurld anonymous character bible

- **Identity principle:** an intentionally anonymous, realistic human silhouette; the character represents a point of view, not a secretly identifiable real person.
- **Allowed framings:** back view; hands; shoulders; torso; cropped silhouette; shadow; controlled profile only if no identifying facial detail is visible; reflections only when they do not reveal a face or identifying feature.
- **Forbidden:** direct face, clear profile identity, face-shaped reflection, identifiable personal details, face swaps, resemblance to an unconsenting real person, deceptive documentary framing or model output that accidentally reveals a face. Reject/regenerate/crop only if this preserves truthful context and does not conceal a material defect.
- **Continuity bible:** neutral fictional character ID; approximate adult age range only when needed; body proportions; wardrobe and material; accessories; posture, gesture, gait; camera distance and framing; movement tempo; light direction/quality; palette; context and forbidden variations. Avoid unnecessary sensitive traits and stereotyped shorthand.
- **Brand demeanor:** calm, purposeful, observant and competent; movement should feel human and restrained, never a claim that the figure is a real customer or employee.
- **Reference asset:** keep a human-approved, rights-cleared canonical frame or written spec. Record its origin, tool, prompt version, permissions and allowed derivatives. Do not use an actual person's image or voice as reference without informed, explicit authorization covering the intended use.

## Voice and character consistency

Define voice role, language/locale, pronunciation, pace, warmth, energy, texture, accent preference and range. Test names, numbers, pauses and emotional shifts. Record the selected tool/model/version, voice source, license/consent, permitted channels/term and disclosure need. A selected synthetic voice is not a human endorsement. Do not imitate a living person's distinctive voice or imply a real spokesperson without authorization and review.

## Prompting and continuity practice

Create a character sheet before generation: stable invariants; allowed scene variation; negative constraints; reference-frame management; crop and safe zones; continuity checks across appearance, wardrobe, proportions, light, movement and camera. Generate shot-specific images/clips; review every frame sequence for identity leakage and temporal artifacts. Avoid asking a system to add text, logos or proof inside generated footage; add approved assets in post.

## Consent, rights and disclosure gate

Before generating a realistic human or voice, confirm: identity is fictional or documented permission is in hand; rights cover generation, editing, distribution, territory, duration and paid use as relevant; no sensitive personal information is used; the prompt and reference assets are authorized; disclosure is assessed for audience understanding, platform requirements and applicable law; provenance is retained; approval is recorded. Rules vary by jurisdiction/platform and change—verify at release, not just at project kickoff.

## Connections

Art direction describes the character's visual grammar; film creates action and continuity; copy must not imply a synthetic character is a genuine customer; distribution considers disclosure and context; governance owns rights, privacy and deception risk; QA checks every visible frame, voice line and derivative.

## Assessment

Design an identity bible and a three-scene continuity review plan. Include prohibited framing, prompt constraints, a rights/consent checklist, voice spec and disclosure decision points. **Pass:** face anonymity is explicit and testable; no real-person likeness/voice without permissions; continuity dimensions are defined; every output has human review and provenance. Any potential face reveal or ambiguous rights status blocks use until resolved.
