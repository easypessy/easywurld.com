## 7. Brand separation and version control

- Keep client profiles, assets, reference material, prompt libraries, customer data and claims in separate project spaces with role-appropriate access.
- Do not train, fine-tune, retrieve from or cross-reference another client's private materials without explicit authorization and appropriate safeguards.
- Do not use one client's logo, slogan, campaign or distinctive creative execution as a template for another client. Reuse the **process**, not their confidential expression or evidence.
- Link every deliverable to the profile version used. If the brand profile changes, record affected assets, owner, review and reapproval.
- Conflicting or stale brand materials are a stop-and-confirm issue; no AI system or junior operator may resolve the conflict by guessing.

## Project onboarding check

Before a new brand enters creative production, a reviewer confirms that the profile names an authorized approver and current source of truth; brand-specific strategy, voice and visual identity are either sourced or marked unknown; claims and assets have evidence/rights owners; character and synthetic-media rules are explicit; unresolved items have owners; and the client workspace is separate from other brands. **Pass:** brand owner has approved the profile and every unknown that materially affects the requested work has a resolution or is a production-blocking open item. Do not treat a blank or unsigned profile as approval.
