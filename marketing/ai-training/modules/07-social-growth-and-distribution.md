# 07 — Social growth & distribution

**Owner:** Distribution / community · **Prerequisites:** 00–06 · **Pass evidence:** Channel plan, adaptation matrix and moderation plan

## What this department must know

Distribution is the system by which useful work reaches an appropriate audience, earns attention and can be acted on. It includes owned, earned, partner, search and paid routes where appropriate. Posting frequency alone is not a strategy. Platform ranking systems are opaque and change; avoid algorithm folklore and promises of reach.

## Channel planning

For each candidate channel, document: audience presence evidence; user intent/context; format and accessibility affordances; discovery route; interaction norms; moderation and response capacity; production cost; policy/risk; destination; measurement limits; and why the channel helps the objective. Re-check the official current format, advertising, branded content, synthetic media and community policies at campaign launch.

## Native adaptation, not duplication

Keep the central promise and evidence consistent, but adapt hook, opening frame, length, aspect ratio, caption, CTA, accessibility and interaction to the use context. Do not crop away a qualification or make a new claim. Static carousel ideas can inform a film but cannot be rendered as video slides; direct a new video independently. Use platform-safe zones and check actual previews. Search/content work should satisfy the query and connect to a useful next step rather than chase keywords mechanically.

## Publishing operations

- Maintain a calendar with objective, owner, approval state, asset version, channel, time zone, destination, tracking link, disclosure and fallback.
- QA final upload and link; verify spelling, subtitles, alt text, thumbnail, safe areas, disclosures and mobile display.
- Use consistent, privacy-respecting tracking. Avoid sensitive data in URLs and do not collect more than needed.
- Make a moderation plan: response owner, response window, FAQ, escalation path, abusive/misinformation handling, crisis contact and archive policy.
- Schedule only at a sustainable cadence. Use audience feedback and team capacity; no universal ideal frequency.
- Reuse only when permissions and rights allow; confirm music, image, talent and platform-use permissions for each new medium/territory.

## Growth loops and community

A useful growth loop connects experience, value, sharing/discovery and continued value. Specify why a user would share, who benefits, what makes sharing easy, and any incentive/disclosure. Do not manufacture comments, engagement pods, fake reviews, deceptive accounts or spam. Community is part of the product experience, not just a reach trick.

## Connections

Strategy chooses audience and objective; copy/design/film adapt expression; identity and governance handle disclosures and rights; analytics defines event links and learns across channels; community feedback can update audience diagnosis and product/offer.

## Assessment

Choose two plausible channels for one brief. Produce a rationale, adaptation matrix, publishing checklist, tracking plan and moderation/escalation plan. **Pass:** channel selection follows audience evidence and objective; adaptations retain truthful message and qualifiers; specifications/policies are date-checked; cadence is operationally sustainable; success metrics distinguish exposure, engagement, action and business outcome; accessibility and community care are planned.
