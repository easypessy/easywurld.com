# 00 — Department operating system

**Owner:** Creative operations · **Prerequisites:** None · **Pass evidence:** Completed brief, source ledger, handoff and decision log

## Purpose

Give strategy, psychology, copy, design, film, synthetic-media, distribution, analytics and governance teams one shared workflow. The system reduces rework by requiring decisions and evidence before execution.

## Outcomes

Learners can: (1) translate a request into an outcome and audience; (2) identify what is known, assumed and missing; (3) assign decision rights; (4) pass usable artifacts between teams; and (5) pause or escalate when a claim, right, safety issue or objective is unclear.

## The Easywurld production loop

**Listen → Diagnose → Decide → Create → Check → Distribute → Learn.**

1. **Intake:** capture requester, business context, deliverable, deadline, budget/resources, intended audience, market/locale, channel, constraints and approver.
2. **Diagnose:** research the audience, category, offer, alternatives and current baseline. Identify evidence gaps and risks.
3. **Decide:** define one primary objective, one audience priority, one central promise, proof, message, CTA and success measure. State what the campaign will not attempt.
4. **Create:** copy, design, film and AI specialists produce from the approved brief, with an explicit version and human owner.
5. **Check:** verify claims, rights, brand, accessibility, platform specs, privacy, disclosures and technical quality. Use a release checklist, not intuition alone.
6. **Distribute:** adapt for channel and context; do not just resize or repost without reason. Keep destination, tracking and moderation ready.
7. **Learn:** compare results with the declared baseline and measurement plan; record uncertainty, unintended effects and the next test.

**Approval gates:** G0 intake accepted; G1 strategy and claims approved; G2 creative concept/story approved; G3 production package and rights cleared; G4 final asset/editorial and platform check; G5 post-publication review. Any reviewer can pause release for a material safety, truth or rights issue. Only the named approver can release.

## Shared brief: required fields

- Project ID, version/date, requester, accountable owner, approver.
- Business context and offer; verified details and exclusions.
- Audience: situation, need, barriers, access/language considerations; evidence/source and confidence.
- Objective: desired audience action and business relevance; primary objective only.
- Message: insight, promise, proof, qualification, objection addressed, CTA and destination.
- Channel, format, market/locale, timing, budget/resources and production constraints.
- Baseline, KPI(s), event definitions, source of data, observation period and attribution limits.
- Brand rules and client-approved assets; rights/consent status; privacy and regulatory questions.
- Assumptions, unknowns, alternatives considered, risks, exclusions and approval history.

## Handoff protocol: BLUF + evidence + next action

Every handoff begins with the **bottom line up front**, then links to the artifact and states: intended use; what is locked vs open; evidence and uncertainty; dependencies; owner; reviewer; due date; acceptance criteria. Receiving team confirms understanding or returns a specific question. Do not pass a long prompt as a substitute for a brief.

## Decision rights

| Decision | Responsible / consultative role | Accountable approver |
|---|---|---|
| Audience, objective, offer and claims | Strategy + research + client contact | Project strategist / authorized client |
| Message and CTA | Copy + strategy + accessibility review | Creative lead / client approver |
| Visual and film concept | Art direction + cinematography + character lead | Creative lead |
| Synthetic likeness, voice, identity and disclosure | AI identity lead + legal/rights reviewer | Rights owner / authorized approver |
| Channel, schedule, tracking and moderation | Distribution + analytics | Campaign owner |
| Final release | Relevant discipline reviewers | Named release approver |

A client may decide its business preference; that does not make unsupported claims permissible. Escalate conflicts rather than silently complying.

## AI use protocol

For each task, record the tool/model and version where practical, task, material inputs, whether personal/confidential data was used, output selected and human verification performed. Do not put sensitive client, customer or personal data into an AI service without documented authorization and appropriate safeguards. AI-generated citations must be opened and checked. Do not present generated imagery, voices, quotes or testimonials as authentic documentary evidence.

## Assessment (pass required)

Given a deliberately incomplete client request, produce a one-page intake, list at least five unknowns, separate facts from assumptions, identify the approval owner, propose a measurement question, and write a handoff to strategy. **Pass:** all required brief fields are addressed or explicitly marked unknown; no invented detail; objective and audience are not conflated; missing approvals are escalated. **Fail/remediate:** invented claims, hidden uncertainty, or proceeding without a named accountable owner.

## Connections

Strategy supplies the brief; psychology explains the audience decision context; copy and creative express the approved message; distribution shapes exposure and context; analytics reports learning; governance has veto on substantiated risk. The loop is iterative: evidence can change strategy, and performance data can reveal a diagnosis error.
