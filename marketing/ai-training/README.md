# Easywurld AI Marketing Intelligence System

**Curriculum version:** 1.0 · **Status:** Foundation curriculum · **Owner:** Easywurld strategy / creative operations  
**Brand principle:** Simplify. Optimize. Grow.

This curriculum trains a cross-functional AI-assisted marketing department to produce sound, useful, on-brand work for Easywurld and its clients. It is a learning and operating system, not a promise of campaign results. **No videos are being generated in this first assignment.** The system is designed so future production begins with evidence, a clear brief and human approval—not with a prompt.

## Start here

1. Read [00 — Department operating system](./modules/00-department-operating-system.md). It establishes shared terms, workflow, gates and handoffs.
2. Complete modules 01–09 in sequence, doing the practice and passing each module's assessment.
3. Use [10 — Capstone, rubric and readiness](./modules/10-capstone-and-readiness.md) to evaluate cross-department work before production access.
4. Consult [11 — Sourcebook and evidence policy](./modules/11-sourcebook-and-evidence-policy.md) when making claims, teaching principles or updating this curriculum.

## Suggested paced rollout

This is a mastery curriculum, not a speed course. A practical first cohort can use a 12-week cadence: week 1 shared operating system; weeks 2–9 one department module per week (01–08); week 10 governance and release controls (09); week 11 capstone (10); week 12 source/citation audit, calibration and remediation (11). Read the sourcebook from the beginning and consult it throughout; week 12 is for audit and upkeep, not first exposure. Build in time for practice, peer review and a reassessment rather than advancing by calendar alone. For each module: short teaching session → worked example → individual practice → peer/specialist critique → revised artifact → pass decision. Schedule more time for research, rights-sensitive or regulated client work.

## Competency progression

- **Level 1 — Explain:** use shared vocabulary; distinguish fact, inference and opinion.
- **Level 2 — Apply:** complete a bounded task with templates and review.
- **Level 3 — Integrate:** make trade-offs across departments and defend them with evidence.
- **Level 4 — Govern:** review, approve, document uncertainty and improve the system.

Learners do not earn production readiness by completing lessons alone. Each capability requires a passing artifact, a human reviewer and a record of any remediation. A person may be approved for one role and not another.

## Shared operating principles

1. **Truth before persuasion.** No fabricated evidence, endorsements, testimonials, guarantees, scarcity or performance claims.
2. **Specific audience, real job.** Define whose situation we are addressing and what useful next step we want to enable.
3. **Evidence with limits.** Label observed data, source claims, assumptions, interpretations and creative hypotheses separately.
4. **Respect autonomy.** Persuasion clarifies value; it does not exploit vulnerability, hide material terms or manufacture pressure.
5. **Human accountability.** AI can assist research, ideation, drafts and production; a named human owns verification, rights and release.
6. **One system, many expressions.** Core promise and brand identity remain consistent; each channel earns its own format and execution.
7. **Test, do not declare.** Treat creative theories as hypotheses and read results in context. No guaranteed virality or invented benchmarks.
8. **Accessible by design.** Readable type, captions, contrast, meaningful alt text, understandable language and usable CTAs are part of quality.

## Easywurld identity baseline for this curriculum

- **Agency:** Easywurld, a marketing and business growth agency.
- **Philosophy:** Simplify. Optimize. Grow.
- **Personality:** intelligent, direct, creative, calm, confident and honest.
- **Core colours supplied for this system:** beige `#FBE7C5`, deep green `#144D3C`, white and black as supporting colours.
- **Anonymous signature character:** realistic human silhouette; never reveal facial identity. Prefer rear views, hands, shoulders, torso, shadows and carefully controlled reflections. Keep wardrobe, proportions, movement, lighting and visual identity consistent. No generated face reveal, no identity-revealing reflection.
- **Client work:** each client gets its own approved brand kit, audience, rights and claims register. Never apply Easywurld identity to client work unless explicitly commissioned.
- **Production constraint:** video is independently directed. Never turn an existing carousel into enlarged video slides. Every future video package must contain the full checklist in module 05.

**Reconciliation note:** Earlier marketing working files in this repository contain a different cream/green/terracotta palette, a different positioning description and a typography-led video style. This curriculum follows the current project brief above as the requested standard. Before using old assets or prompts for production, a brand owner must reconcile them against the approved identity and update or explicitly grandfather them. This curriculum does not silently rewrite historical assets.

## How to use and maintain the documents

- Use the modules as a training sequence and reference manual. Templates belong in the relevant module; copy them into project folders rather than editing the master curriculum for each campaign.
- For each client, create a project workspace with brief, source ledger, claims register, asset rights log, decision log, production package and post-campaign learning memo.
- Revisit platform policies, laws, tool capabilities, media formats and synthetic-media disclosure rules at project kickoff and before release; they change. Record the date checked and authoritative source.
- Review this curriculum quarterly, and immediately after a safety incident, material policy change or repeated assessment failure. Version any substantive change and preserve its rationale.
