## How to attach the files

Every file in `assets/` is named **`HHMM_POSTID_position-of-total`** — for example `0700_EW-D01-S01_3of5.jpg` is the 07:00 post, slide 3 of 5. Sort the folder by name and it is already in posting order.

- **Carousels:** attach the five slides in the numbered order (`1of5` → `5of5`). `04_CAROUSELS.md` lists each carousel slide by slide with what it says, and `05_CONTACT_SHEETS.pdf` shows all five side by side, numbered, so you can check the arrangement at a glance before you post.
- **Stories:** three frames, `1of3` → `3of3`, in that order.
- **Videos:** one MP4, nothing to arrange — upload as is.
- **LinkedIn:** one PDF per document post; the native video slot uses the same MP4 as the feed.
