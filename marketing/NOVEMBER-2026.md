# November 2026 — the entertain-first month

**30 days · Sunday 1 November → Monday 30 November 2026 · Lagos time**

This is not a replacement for the calendars in `launch-content/`. It is the **one that replaces
them**, and it is deliberately small: one anchor idea per day, with volume built by restructuring
that idea per platform — which is what `POSTING_PLAN.md` already told you to do.

Read [`storytelling/THE-ITCH.md`](storytelling/THE-ITCH.md) first. This month runs on it.

---

## 1. The thesis, in one line

> **Entertain everybody. Teach whoever stayed. Let whoever is ready buy — quietly.**

Three jobs, three tiers, one job per post. Never all three at once.

| Tier | Share | What it does | What it never does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ENTERTAIN** | 60% | Reaches strangers | Sells |
| **TEACH** | 30% | Turns strangers into people who trust you | Pitches |
| **SELL** | 10% | Lets the ready ones pay | Chases |

That ratio comes from Adley Kinsman's own rule — 60%+ of output should be top-of-funnel — and it
is the correction to the last plan, which was 100% teaching and offer. A thousand teaching posts
shown to 200 people changes nothing. Sixty entertaining posts shown to 200,000 changes everything.

---

## 2. The four shows

Four repeating formats. That is the whole slate — big accounts post in one to three formats
endlessly, because novelty is expensive and consistency compounds.

| Show | Tier | What it is | Why it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| **FIX THIS** | Entertain | 25–30s teardown of a real Lagos business page or profile | A stranger's business being fixed is the most watchable thing you can make, and it proves competence without claiming it |
| **WE SAID NO** | Entertain | A story where Easywurld refused money, cancelled something, or told the truth at a cost | Nobody in Lagos is posting this. It cannot be faked, which is exactly why it is worth making |
| **THE STEAL** | Teach | One thing they can copy today, in under 30 seconds | Saves and shares; makes you the person who gives things away |
| **THE HONEST AD** | Sell | The offer, sold with the price *and* the limit stated | Honesty is your only differentiator; make it the ad |

Plus **THE MEME**, once a week, from the 37 already written in `launch-content/social-command/MEME_BANK.md`.

**Naming:** say the show name out loud at the start or put it on screen. "Fix This. Episode 6."
A named show is a reason to follow; an unnamed video is a reason to scroll.

## 5. Turning 1 anchor into your volume

The 37-a-day plan was never wrong about *volume*. It was wrong about **originality per post**.
Do this instead:

```
1 anchor idea
   ├── TikTok        → spoken, 20–35s, hook first, captions burned in
   ├── Instagram     → same idea as 5 slides, or a 30s reel
   ├── Facebook      → 80–150 words of conversation, ends in a question
   └── LinkedIn      → the same story, written about the work, no emoji
```

That is 4 posts from one idea, and the idea is the only part that costs you anything. 1 anchor a
day × 4 platforms = 120 posts a month, all original in the only way that matters, all traceable
with one code.

**If you want the 30-a-day shape back:** remake the anchor. Same hook, different delivery — that
is literally what `@personalbrandlaunch0`'s "Same Hook, Different Delivery" post is about, and
what Adley does by shooting four openings of one video. Remakes are counted as posts in the
tracker, because they cost you the same effort.

## 6. The buy path — "allow whoever wants to buy"

The user's own words. Made concrete:

| Step | What | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Bio link → `https://website-builder-portfolio.vercel.app/bundle?src=ig-bio` | Every profile |
| 2 | Pinned comment on every offer post: the same link, nothing else | 3 sell posts + 3 teach posts |
| 3 | One word in the video: "BUNDLE", "PLAYBOOK", "SAMPLE" | Sell posts only |
| 4 | Anyone who comments gets a DM **within the hour** | This is the conversion |
| 5 | The DM is one line and a payment link | `client/src/data/payment.ts` |
| 6 | No countdowns, no "only 3 left", no fake scarcity | Ever |

**The rule that makes entertainment-first selling work:** the sell posts are *just as
entertaining* as the entertain posts. The only difference is that one honest sentence at the end
names the price and the limit. If the ad is worse content than the story, it does not deserve the
audience the story built.

## 8. How the month is measured

Everything here plugs into the system already built:

- Every link carries `?src=` — so `ig-offer`, `tt-fixthis`, `li-trust` arrive in the WhatsApp message
  as `[ref: …]`.
- Every Friday: add a row per channel × show to `measurement/weekly-tracker.csv`, then `npm run score`.
- **The new comparison that matters:** the tracker's `pillar` column maps the tiers like this —

| Tier | `pillar` values |
|---|---|
| ENTERTAIN | `story`, `meme`, `hottake` |
| TEACH | `teaching`, `trust` |
| SELL | `offer` |

So after two weeks the scorecard tells you, in numbers, whether **FIX THIS** or **THE STEAL**
produces more conversations — which is the question you actually asked. If entertain posts reach
10× the people but sell nothing, and teach posts reach 1/10th but convert 5×, the scorecard will
show it and you can move the ratio from 60/30/10 to 50/40/10 — with evidence instead of a feeling.

> **A warning about the first two weeks.** Entertain posts will look like they are failing,
> because reach does not produce conversations immediately — it produces *followers*, and
> followers convert later. Judge entertainment on **follows and saves per post**, judge teaching
> on **conversations per post**, and judge selling on **revenue per post**. Same sheet, three
> different lenses.

## 9. What is deliberately unfinished

You said you would keep adding. So this month is a frame with room in it:

- **Only four shows.** There is space for a fifth — the natural candidate is a weekly
  "ask a Lagos business owner one question" interview, which is the cheapest way to get other
  people to share your videos.
- **No guest or client stories yet.** Once you have one real project, "WE SAID NO" gains a
  companion: "WE BUILT THIS", with the client's actual words.
- **The November numbers are unknown.** The targets in the main plan (20 offer sales, 3 projects)
  stay as the aim. The scorecard is what replaces guessing — including guessing at targets.
