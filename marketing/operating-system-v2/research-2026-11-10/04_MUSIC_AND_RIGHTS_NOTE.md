# 04 — Music and rights: parked, with the facts that matter when it unparks

**Decision on 10 November 2026: no songs in this delivery.** Nothing here is a music recommendation, a
licence, or clearance for any track. This page exists for two reasons: so the question is not lost, and so that
whoever picks it up later does not start from a wrong assumption.

## Why it is parked rather than answered

The request can be read two ways, and they need different work:

1. **Licensed music suggestions** — tracks an owner could legally put under a Reel or a carousel video. This is a
   rights question first and a taste question second. It needs a licence, not a playlist.
2. **Original song concepts** — lyrics, hooks or a jingle written for Easywurld. This is a writing and
   composition task, and it still needs a rights position before it is used in advertising.

Both are deliverable later. Neither belongs inside a fact-checked carousel delivery that has no rendered video,
no confirmed offer and no named release approver. Shipping "song ideas" alongside compliance research would mix
two different confidence levels in one package, which is exactly how unverified material gets quoted later.

## The facts that will govern it, already in the register

| Fact | What it says | Why it matters for songs |
|---|---|---|
| `PL-META-02` | "Use of music for commercial or non-personal purposes in particular is prohibited unless you have obtained appropriate licenses." Content with someone else's music "may be blocked, muted or removed". | A brand Page or a business Reel is commercial. Being in the in-app library is not the same as being licensed for a brand. |
| `PL-TT-01` | Business accounts are pointed at TikTok's Commercial Music Library, whose commercial scope is described as TikTok-only, with per-track placements. | A track cleared for TikTok is not automatically cleared for Reels, YouTube Shorts, or the client's own website. |
| `NG-FCCPA-04` | You may not falsely represent sponsorship, approval or affiliation. | "As heard in…", an artist tag, or a "featured" badge needs paperwork, not vibes. |
| `REPO-01` | Fonts are referenced, not licensed — no licence record exists in the repository. | If typefaces are unclear, music will be worse: it has two copyrights (composition and recording), not one. |

## What would unblock it

- [ ] Say which of the two meanings is wanted: **licensed suggestions** or **original concepts**.
- [ ] If licensed: pick the platform set first (TikTok only? Meta only? both plus website?), because scope
      follows placement, and no single library covers all of them.
- [ ] If original: confirm who owns the composition and the recording, and get that in writing before any
      recording is used in paid or organic brand content.
- [ ] Decide the proof file to keep per track: licence or grant summary, receipt, track identifiers, platforms,
      territory, term.
- [ ] Name the person who signs off music, separate from the person who writes the post.

## Interim rule for the December drafts

Any draft that would carry music stays **visual-first**: carousel, photo, or silent video with on-screen text.
That is consistent with the existing feed direction (carousel-only core feed) and avoids inventing a rights
position for 330 packages that have no rendered assets yet.

**Status: DEFERRED — not researched to licence level, not recommended, not cleared.**
