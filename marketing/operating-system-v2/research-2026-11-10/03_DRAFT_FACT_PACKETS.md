# Draft fact packets — which research each series must carry

**State: research attached, approval not granted.** These packets map the research register onto the eleven draft series. They exist so a reviewer can see, per slot, which real rule or policy the copy leans on and what still has to be confirmed by a named person.

A packet is not a clearance. It records the source behind a claim; it does not decide that the claim is used well, that the design is readable, or that the post may be published.

| Fact | Why not | What would unblock it |
|---|---|---|
| `NG-ARCON-06` | Read from commentary, not from ARCON's own guidelines. Confirm current forms, fees and exemptions directly with ARCON before advising a client. | Read ARCON's current vetting guidelines, or a signed note from a lawyer or registered practitioner confirming the procedure and fees. |
| `NG-ARCON-07` | This is the single most consequential and least settled item in this register. Outlets disagree on scope, amounts and court outcomes; the BusinessDay piece is partly paywalled. Treat as an open question for the owner/legal adviser, not a quotable fact. | A dated primary statement from ARCON or a court, plus the owner's decision on whether to vet before posting. Until then, no draft may state a settled scope. |
| `NG-FCCPC-01` | Use as evidence that enforcement happens in practice. It is not a prediction about any specific business or sector. | Not needed for use as background. Cite it as a Commission release, never as a prediction about a specific sector. |
| `PL-TT-01` | TikTok's own terms were not retrieved for this register (blocked on 2026-11-10). Confirm in TikTok's current Music Terms before relying on any placement claim. | Read TikTok's current Music Terms and the per-track "usable placements" for the specific track. |
| `PL-LI-01` | LinkedIn Help would not load on 2026-11-10. Confirm the role and format limits in the actual account before promising any timetable. | Check the admin role and the format limit inside the actual Page, then record the screenshot date. |
| `MKT-01` | A single commercial blog's ranges, not a statistical survey or a regulator's figure. It supports "inside a published range" and nothing stronger. It must never be used to imply a saving or a struck-through price. | A primary source read and dated, or a named reviewer |
