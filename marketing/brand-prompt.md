4. DIY packs and custom projects are different products. State the boundary.
5. The current source lists each pack at **₦5,000**. Confirm price, contents, payment and delivery before a sales post.
6. Do not publish a private CV, identity document, customer record or payment detail.
7. Use only an owner-approved likeness and voice, or a provider avatar licensed for this commercial use. The fictional demo founder is not the owner.
8. Disclose realistic AI presenters and synthetic speech. Enable the platform setting.
9. Use own/licensed music or the platform’s commercial library. A trending personal-account sound is not automatically cleared for a brand.

12. Memes must be original, relevant and never punch down at customers.

Copy this into a suitable avatar/video tool **after** supplying the approved source and checking consent/commercial terms. Fill every bracket from the brief. Do not publish bracketed instructions.

[IF I RAN / MYTH COURT / OFFER B / MEET EASYWURLD]

Chest-up framing, eye-level camera, soft natural light, quiet warm-neutral room.
Steady eye contact, small natural gestures, calm conversational pace. No waving
intro, exaggerated nodding, sales shouting, fake podcast microphone or glamour
lighting. Keep the face in the central safe area. Do not bake captions, logos,
prices or claims into the generated footage; add those in the editor.

SCRIPT — speak exactly this approved text, with no extra claims:
[PASTE THE POST'S FULL SCRIPT]

VISUAL INTENTION
Hook: [EXACT HOOK]
Supporting cutaway: [ONE RELEVANT WORKSHEET, SCREEN DEMO OR CHECKLIST]
Final action: [ONE CTA]

Target [DURATION FROM BRIEF]. Generate short sections if the tool cannot maintain

No testimonials, invented results, customer data, countdowns or guarantees.

**Voice:** first test the approved cloned voice or a licensed synthetic voice the owner approves. Do not claim a generic voice is the owner’s. Check “Easywurld”, “Lagos”, “naira”, “SEO”, “WhatsApp” and every price aloud.

**If the source is missing:** use the post’s non-avatar fallback. The niche, meme, offer-A, copy and many intro posts do not need a synthetic face.

## 7. Editing specification

- Master video: **1080×1920, 9:16, MP4/H.264, 30 fps**, clear voice, captions burned in.
- Carousel: **1080×1350, 4:5**, five PNG slides. Static: one 1080×1350 PNG.
- First frame: the problem and the face/demo already visible. No logo animation.
- Captions: short phrase lines, high contrast, correct spelling and stable timing. Avoid flashing word-by-word karaoke.
- House safe area: keep essential text roughly within x=80–850 and y=260–1450 on a 1080×1920 frame. This is a conservative guide, **not an official TikTok guarantee**. Preview against the actual UI.
- Put the AI label around x=80, y=220, above the main caption block. Keep it visible throughout.
- Use a cutaway or small reframe when the idea changes. Do not zoom on every sentence.
- No long end card. Keep the final action inside the useful content.
- Music is optional. Keep it below the voice and confirm commercial and cross-platform rights.

## 8. Caption and CTA rules

Hook → useful explanation or example → one action → relevant hashtags. Include the AI disclosure for synthetic presenters/voices and the sample label for demos. Use three to five specific hashtags.

Do not stack “comment, save, share, follow, visit and buy” in one post. Use the CTA in the brief. For a DIY offer, name one keyword and tell the buyer to confirm current order details. For a custom project, ask for the business link and the problem.

Platform captions are generated in `content-plan.json` and the CSV. Rewrite them only if the platform adaptation genuinely requires it; keep the hook, facts and one CTA.
