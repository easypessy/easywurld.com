# The Operator — anonymous founder persona for AI talking heads

**Purpose:** one consistent, calm, faceless presenter for the 62 AI talking-head slots (Marketing Myth Court and Offer Spotlight B) and the rotating `Meet Easywurld` slot.
**Status:** concept approved, art and voice samples produced. **Not cleared for publication until the owner confirms likeness-free use and reads the disclosure section below.**
**Assets:** `marketing/assets/avatar/` — `operator-hero.png`, `operator-presence.png`, `operator-desk.png`, `voice-sample-myth.mp3`.

---

## 1. The idea in one line

> A man you never fully see, who is never in a hurry, and who always seems to know one thing you do not.

The face stays hidden. The **life** is what is shown: the late desk, the notebook, the rain on the window, the phone face down, the calendar with days crossed out. Viewers do not follow him because they know him. They follow because they want the life he seems to have — quiet, competent, in control, working for himself.

This is deliberately the opposite of the loud Lagos "buy now" presenter. The restraint is the brand.

## 2. Why this works as content (not just as decoration)

| Viewer reaction | What causes it | What it buys us |
|---|---|---|
| "Who is this?" | Face never shown, no name used | Comments and repeat visits — the cheapest reach there is |
| "I want that life" | Night desk, city window, notebook, calm | Aspiration, which is what actually sells services |
| "He is not desperate" | Slow speech, no urgency, no shouting | Authority. Price resistance drops when the seller is unhurried |
| "He tells the truth" | Verdicts with limits, no guaranteed results | Trust, which is what makes a DM happen |

Mystery pulls attention. Restraint keeps it. Neither works if the person starts bragging.

## 3. Hard rules

1. **No face. Ever.** Back of head, silhouette, shoulder, hand, jaw edge in shadow. If a frame reveals eyes or a full face, delete the frame.
2. **No name, no age, no biography claims.** Refer to "the studio", "me", "we". The audience can speculate; we never confirm.
3. **No invented wealth.** No supercars, no champagne, no fake office tower, no borrowed lifestyle. The aesthetic is *working late*, not *flaunting*. Money signals come from calm and craft, not props.
4. **No invented client stories, results, revenue or testimonials.** Vignettes are about the work and the mood, never about a "client who made ₦2m".
5. **No menace.** The reference is a low, certain, courteous voice — not threats. Nothing about "destroying competitors", "crushing", or violence. The power is composure.
6. **Disclose.** Every clip carries `AI presenter · Easywurld` on screen and the platform AI setting on. Never imply a real filmed face.
7. **One CTA.** Always the WhatsApp keyword or the site. Never two asks.

## 4. The look (image direction)

**Palette:** the brand greens and cream, held low and dark. Deep green `#0A4937`, leaf `#27473A`, cream `#FAE7C7` as the only light, terracotta `#9E4E2C` as a rare accent.

**Wardrobe:** deep green overshirt or plain dark tee, sleeves pushed up, one plain watch. Nothing branded, no logos.

**Set:** wooden desk, brass or amber lamp, open leather notebook with a pen, phone face down, glass of dark tea, monstera, rain-streaked window with city bokeh, sneakers by the door, wall calendar with days crossed out.

**Light:** warm amber key from one side, cool blue screen glow from below. 60–70% of the frame in shadow.

**Lens and crop:** 35–50mm, shallow depth of field, vertical 9:16, subject occupying the right or left third with negative space for captions.

**Motion:** almost none. A slow breath, a hand turning a page, steam. No fidgeting, no gestures at camera.

Regenerate stills with the prompts in `assets/avatar/README.md`.

## 5. The voice (delivery card)

The voice is the product. Get this right and the hidden face stops feeling like a gimmick.

| Attribute | Setting |
|---|---|
| Register | Low, warm, chesty, slightly behind the beat |
| Pace | Slow. ~120–135 words per minute. Never rush a sentence |
| Pauses | Full stop = real pause. 0.4–0.7s between sentences, 1s before a verdict |
| Volume | Conversational, as if across a small table. No projection |
| Emotion | Amused certainty. He is not surprised by your problem |
| Habit | Says the hard thing plainly, then stops. Does not soften it |
| Forbidden | Shouting, upsell energy, "guys", "link in bio", rising salesperson pitch, fake urgency |

**Pacing pattern to copy:** *"Your page is not ugly."* … (beat) … *"It is just saying the wrong thing."* … (beat) … *"That is a cheaper problem to fix than you think."*

**Pronunciation:** naira amounts as words ("five thousand naira"), "WhatsApp" as one word, "Easywurld" as *easy-world*, "SEO" as letters, "Lagos" flat.

**Tool notes:** use one locked voice model for all clips; do not re-roll the voice per post or the persona breaks. Keep a saved preset (stability high, similarity high, style low) so every week sounds like the same person.

## 6. Script formula (28–35 seconds)

| Beat | Time | Job | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cold open | 0:00–0:02 | One flat, surprising sentence. No greeting | "Nobody buys because of your logo." |
| The turn | 0:02–0:06 | Name the real cause | "They buy because they understood the offer in four seconds." |
| The receipt | 0:06–0:20 | One concrete detail, one number we can defend | "One headline. One price. One next step." |
| The verdict | 0:20–0:27 | Say what is true, including the limit | "That is not branding. That is a decision you have not made yet." |
| CTA | 0:27–0:32 | One ask, calm | "Message PLAYBOOK if you want the checklist. Five thousand naira. DIY." |

Rules: one idea per clip. No intro. No "hey guys". No logo animation at the open. Land the verdict before the CTA, never after.

## 8. The 30-day story arc for `Meet Easywurld` (20:30)

Do not tell a fake biography. Tell a *sequence of observations from inside the work*, one per night. Five chapters, six nights each:

1. **Nights 1–6 — Why the studio exists.** What small businesses around you keep getting wrong. Nothing personal.
2. **Nights 7–12 — How we work.** Understand, simplify, build, optimise. One principle per night, shown on the desk.
3. **Nights 13–18 — What we refuse to do.** No fake results, no vanity dashboards, no posting for the sake of it. Refusals build more trust than promises.
4. **Nights 19–24 — The tools.** AI as an assistant with a human approval step, never as a replacement for judgement.
5. **Nights 25–30 — What we want to be hired for.** The specific jobs, the specific businesses, the honest boundary between DIY packs and custom work.

Each night keeps the same set, the same voice and the same lighting. Only the notebook page changes. Repetition is what turns a hidden man into a recognisable brand.

> **Truth check before recording:** if a line describes a real event, the owner must confirm it happened. If it is not verified, keep the line about the *work* (a pattern you see), not about *you* (a story you lived). Never invent a client, a number or a result.

## 9. Production pipeline

1. Pick the brief: `marketing/briefs/EW-Dxx-Sxx.md`.
2. Generate the voice audio from the exact script with the locked preset. Keep the text file beside the audio.
3. Animate with a licensed avatar/video tool. Feed it `operator-hero.png` or `operator-presence.png` **as a scene reference only** — no provider face-swap is needed, because no face is shown. Back-of-head and silhouette clips are safer, cheaper and more on-brand than a lip-synced face.
4. Where a lip-synced face is unavoidable, use a licensed provider avatar, never a real person's photo without written permission.
5. Edit in CapCut: 9:16, 1080×1920, 30fps. Trim the open, put captions in the safe area, one slow punch-in per beat, keep the room tone under the voice.
6. Burn in `AI presenter · Easywurld` plus the real logo end-card (last 1.5s).
7. QA: face never visible, one CTA, price and pack contents correct, trend wording removed if unverified, disclosure on, audio levels −14 to −16 LUFS.
8. Upload as Draft. Record the actual URL in the app after publishing.

## 10. Fallbacks (when the persona cannot be produced)

- Static text card in the same set photography, with the script as caption.
- Screen demo with the script as voice-over and no presenter.
- The existing series cover art for that slot.

Missing a slot is always better than breaking the persona.

## 11. Disclosure and permission

- The persona is a **fictional, non-identifiable presenter**. It is not a real person's likeness, and it must never be described as the owner's face.
- Do not accept "this is me" framing in captions. Say `AI presenter · Easywurld`, script reviewed by the studio.
- If the owner ever supplies a real photo and wants a real likeness, that requires written permission and a new consent step; this document is the fallback that needs neither.
- Commercial terms: check the avatar/voice provider's licence for business use, retention, export and voice cloning on every tool before paying.
