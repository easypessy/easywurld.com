# Easywurld visual direction review — then authorize content drafting

Open the [85-layout review gallery](index.html) or the Google Slides review deck (Drive link recorded in the handoff). The deck has one template per slide, not 85 finished posts. Compare the cover, two **distinct** internal layouts and conclusion of every carousel family; then stories and LinkedIn families. Use this file to record specific revisions.

## Review and mark up

1. **First impression:** does each of the 11 series look like the same agency but have its own recognizable editorial visual concept? Which family or slide role should change? Refer to **deck slide number + family + role**, e.g. “slide 4, Diagnosis, interior-b: make the process path larger”.
2. **Readability:** check long headlines, information density and both beige/green contrast directions at your actual phone size. SVG proofs use font fallbacks; licensed Lora/Canva Sans can change line breaks.
3. **Platform safety:** in the gallery choose **Show safe areas**. Look at TikTok right rail/bottom caption, story top/bottom bars and reserved sticker zones, feed crop and LinkedIn preview. The overlay is conservative, not a guarantee. Name any screen where the hook, important qualification, CTA or prompt could be covered by a button or caption.
4. **Truth/rights:** offer samples contain `[VERIFY CURRENT TERMS]`; proof/strategy samples are fictional; personal LinkedIn text is proposed, not a real experience. Do not authorize use of an old offer price, client proof, photo, logo or screenshot without rights/source confirmation.
5. **Feedback:** list Keep / Revise / Drop by exact template name and note what should change. Avoid asking for a generic palette swap; explain the intended buyer decision or reading problem.

This is **approval of template direction and permission to draft content (G0–G2)**, not approval of future factual claims, licensed art, real account roles or G3/G4 final assets. If current account queues or publication times are unknown, say `TIME PENDING`; drafting can continue without pretending a schedule exists. After each batch is rewritten, human copy/rights/visual QC must gate actual distribution.
