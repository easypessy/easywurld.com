# November 2026 — the full 30 days

**330 core posts · 11 per day · TikTok + Instagram + Facebook · no LinkedIn**
Sunday 1 November → Monday 30 November 2026 · Lagos time (WAT)

This is the plan for the whole month. Days 1 and 2 in this folder are already **written to the
final word** (`content/days-01-02.json` → `MONTH_POSTS.md`, the phone app and the CSV).
Days 3–30 below are planned hook by hook, ready to be written out in the same standard.

---

## The shape

| | |
|---|---|
| Core ideas per day | **11** |
| Published posts per day | **33** (each idea → TikTok + Instagram + Facebook, written separately) |
| Month total | **330 core ideas → 990 published posts** |
| Tier split | **6–7 entertain · 3 teach · 1 sell** every day (≈60 / 30 / 10) |
| Platform weighting | **TikTok** carries the entertainment (47.8M adult reach, +43% a year). **Facebook** carries the teaching and selling (72% of Nigerians use it). **Instagram** gets the best of both (10M reach, highest intent in Lagos). |

### The eleven slots, every day

| Slot | Time | Show | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 07:00 | **FIX THIS** — a teardown | entertain |
| 2 | 08:30 | **ONE THING** — a meme or observation | entertain |
| 3 | 10:00 | **THE STEAL** — one useful thing | teach |
| 4 | 11:30 | **HOT TAKE** — a position | entertain |
| 5 | 13:00 | **TRUE STORY** — a real story | entertain |
| 6 | 14:30 | **THE STEAL** — second lesson | teach |
| 7 | 16:00 | **ONE THING** | entertain |
| 8 | 17:30 | **STREET** — ask people on the street | entertain |
| 9 | 19:00 | **THE HONEST AD** — the offer | sell |
| 10 | 20:15 | **WE SAID NO** — a story that costs us | entertain |
| 11 | 21:30 | **THE STEAL** / **FREE REVIEW** | teach |

Platform times differ from these — the builder assigns TikTok, Instagram and Facebook their own
times from `build/build-kit.mjs`. These eleven are the *idea* slots.

### The code on every link

`{channel}-{show}` — `tt-fixthis`, `ig-steal`, `fb-honestad`, `wa-meme`.
Every WhatsApp message leaves the site carrying `[ref: tt-fixthis]`. See
[`../measurement/README.md`](../measurement/README.md).

## How this gets built

```bash
# Days already written, to the final word
launch-content/november/content/days-01-02.json

# Add days as days-03-04.json, days-05-07.json ... then rebuild
node launch-content/november/build/build-kit.mjs
```

That produces, every time:

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `EASYWURLD_POST_COMMANDER.html` | The phone app. One file, offline, copy button on every block, tick marks saved on the phone. |
| `MONTH_POSTS.md` | Every post in long form — hook, beats, voiceover, slides, caption, hashtags. |
| `content_calendar.csv` | One dated row per scheduled post, 33 a day. |

## The rules this month runs on

1. **One job per post.** Entertain, teach, or sell — never two.
2. **The sell post names a price and a limit**, every single time.
3. **No invented proof, no fake scarcity, no guarantees.** The validator fails the build for all three.
4. **The entertainment can be about anything, but it is made for a business owner.** Wide appeal, narrow audience.
5. **Every link carries a code**, so the tracker can say which show paid.

> Any subject, one form. That is how you get reach without becoming a comedy account.
