you open is always current. The ten-day bulk zip
(`Easywurld_November_Days_01-10_UPGRADED-bulk.zip`) stays in the same folder for

## What is upgraded in SET_B

- Every narration is rewritten to the villain / hero / ally structure: the problem
  is named early, the daily damage is shown, Easywurld steps in and is honest that
  it cannot win alone, and the client's yes is the turning point.
- The audience's own words, rewritten — never copied. Bank: `marketing/AUDIENCE_PHRASES.md`.
- Finished videos are 35-50 seconds, 1080×1920, with voiceover, music bed, burned
  captions and the disclosure chip rendered in. No editing needed.
- LinkedIn is 4 posts a day on the company page and 3 a day on the personal
  profile, never the same text twice in a day.

## Finished-video status

| Days | Finished videos (voiceover + music + captions rendered) |
|---|---|
| 1-4 | **All 20 slots rendered** — post as they are |
| 5-30 | Packs are complete; the video slots still carry their poster until that batch renders. Two days are added per production batch, and only those `Day_XX.zip` files change |

The day sheet always names the exact file it expects, so a day is never ambiguous:
if it lists an MP4, the MP4 is in `assets/`.

## Order of work each morning

1. Open today's `Day_XX.zip` in SET_B.
2. Read `00_DAY_SHEET.md` top to bottom — it is in click order.
3. Attach the file it names, paste the caption from `02_CAPTIONS.md`, set the time,
   switch on the AI label where the sheet says so.
4. LinkedIn from `01_LINKEDIN.md`: body in the post, link in the **first comment**.
5. Nothing publishes itself. Every row is `Draft` until a human schedules it.
