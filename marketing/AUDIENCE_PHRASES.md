# Audience phrases — the running doc

Standing instruction: real phrases beat invented ones. This file is the bank. Every
upgrade cycle appends to it; every piece of content borrows from it, **rewritten in our
own words — never copied from a post or comment**.

Cycle 1 — 2 November 2026. Sources: Nairaland business threads ("DM For Price: Why Do
Online Vendors Do This?", "No Single Business Is Lucrative In Nigeria"), r/Nigeria
scam/trust threads, BusinessDay owner interviews, and local-visibility write-ups.

| "TikTok page that deletes comments and ghosts you after payment" (r/Nigeria) | the ghosting villain; feeds the follow-up series |

## Objections to answer before they are asked

- "Is this a website build?" → no: DIY blueprint and templates.
- "Does this automate my WhatsApp?" → no: scripts, tracker, routine; nothing auto-sent.
- "₦5,000 for only this?" → say the boundary before the price is challenged.
- "I will get back to you." → the follow-up system exists for exactly this line.

## Rules for using this bank

1. Rewrite, never quote. A phrase earns its place when a reader says "this is my week".
2. One villain per piece, named early, with the damage shown in daily life.
3. Easywurld steps in as the hero **and says it cannot win alone**.
4. The client's yes is the turning point — give them a role, not a receipt.
5. Light Naija touches once or twice per piece, at warm moments only.
