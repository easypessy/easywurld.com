# FIX THIS · the shop where nobody gets an answer

A worked script in the **kishōtenketsu** shape from `marketing/craft/03-STORYTELLING.md`.
Check it with:

```
npm run story -- marketing/storytelling/examples/the-silence.md
```

## The four parts, mapped

| Part | Beat | Line |
|---|---|---|
| **Ki** — the world as it looks | `@I` + `@T` | A normal page, scrolled normally. Nothing appears wrong. |
| **Shō** — the pattern deepens | `@C` | The questions exist, but every answer moves into a private chat. |
| **Ten** — the turn | `@H` | The fix is not a better post. It is a date next to the price. |
| **Ketsu** — meaning lands | `@SCRATCH` | The chat has a reason to reopen. |

No villain, no fight, nothing at stake — and it still holds, because the turn re-frames the
ordinary thing. That is what makes this shape work for the Entertain tier.

Keep the beat lines clean. Anything you write on an `@` line counts toward the word budget.

@tier entertain
@seconds 30

@I 0:00 Phone screen: a small shop's page, scrolling slowly. Normal products, normal captions.

@T 0:03 This shop gets forty price questions a month.

@C 0:07 Not one is answered in public. Every reply says "DM us", so the price moves into a private chat — and that chat goes quiet. The customer says the interest just offs, and the shop never learns.

@H 0:13 Here is the fix. Price under the post, but the date next to it: ₦8,500, ready Friday. Therefore the next DM asks about colour, not cost.

@SCRATCH 0:24 Now the quiet chat has a reason to reopen.

@LOOP 0:27 Tomorrow: the same silence, on a food vendor's status.
